A Category-Selective Encryption Framework for Privacy Protection of UAV Images

  • 作者Ting Yan, Minghui Shang, Haolang Sun, Xu Wang, Yamin Han, Mingze He
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-26T16:46:17Z
  • 分类cs.CR, cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Imagery captured by unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) systems increasingly raises privacy concerns, as UAV aerial surveillance routinely captures sensitive objects such as pedestrians and vehicles alongside ordinary scene content. Existing privacy-preserving approaches typically employ class-agnostic saliency detection to indiscriminately obfuscate all visually salient regions, leading to unnecessary information loss and hindering downstream scene analysis. This paper proposes a category-selective encryption framework for privacy-preserving UAV surveillance. The core advance over prior class-agnostic approaches is semantic-level selectivity: the system first recognizes what is present in the scene via an 80-class detector, then encrypts only user-specified sensitive categories while leaving non-sensitive objects and background intact for downstream analytics. The framework supports two complementary encryption schemes: Thumbnail-Preserving Encryption (TPE), which applies adaptive pixelation with a learnable blend parameter and recovers content through a jointly trained Enhanced U-Net, and XOR stream cipher encryption, which offers parameter-free, mathematically exact reversibility. The architectural design, composite loss formulation, and joint training methodology are presented in detail, establishing a foundation for practical privacy-preserving UAV surveillance that balances protection strength and scene utility.

VisGeo-PipeTrack: A Visual Geometric Servo Control Framework with MPC-RCAPID Control for UAV-Based Dynamic Pipes Tracking

  • 作者Zhenyang Gao, Jianlin Chen, Yang Yu, Honghao Shan, Jiale Gao, Ziyan Jia
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-26T12:34:40Z
  • 分类cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

While unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) are increasingly deployed for pipeline inspection, prevailing research predominantly assumes static pipes. In real-world industrial environments, pipes often exhibit dynamic movements, such as vibrations. This imposes more stringent requirements on UAVs' adaptive control and computational efficiency than in static scenarios. Therefore, this paper proposes a servo control framework named VisGeo-PipeTrack, which utilizes geometric constraints to enable UAV tracking of dynamic pipes. To achieve efficient UAV positioning, YOLOv5 is employed to detect labels on the pipe. The positions are derived from visual geometric constraints based on these detected labels to enable precise localization. To address tracking control challenges, a model predictive control based real-time corrected adaptive PID (MPC-RCAPID) controller is proposed. This controller employs the Recursive Least Squares method for real-time model estimation and integrates rolling horizon optimization to dynamically adjust PID parameters. Experiments conducted on both the AirSim simulator and the Tello UAV platform validate the effectiveness of the proposed framework. Simulation comparisons with standard PID, fractional-order PID, adaptive PID, and fuzzy PID demonstrate that MPC-RCAPID achieves the shortest settling time (3.26 s) and the lowest average tracking error (3.52%). In physical experiments on the Tello platform, compared with standard PID control, MPC-RCAPID reduces the average tracking error by 55.52%, 26.72%, and 26.52% across the three test dimensions. These results demonstrate that VisGeo-PipeTrack facilitates efficient autonomous tracking in dynamic pipe environments.

  • 作者Wenchao Liu, Weijie Yuan, Jun Wu, Jinke Ren, Xuhui Zhang, Changsheng You
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-26T08:58:38Z
  • 分类eess.SP

Low-altitude wireless networks (LAWNs) require uncrewed aerial vehicle (UAV) communication systems to adapt to large-scale propagation changes and fast local channel fluctuations. Fluid antennas (FAs) provide an additional degree of freedom (DoF) by reconfiguring antenna positions within a compact aperture. This article investigates FA-enabled UAV communication systems from the perspectives of fundamentals, design challenges, and future research directions. We first introduce a dual-mobility perspective, in which UAV trajectory planning provides macro-mobility for large-scale air-to-ground geometry adaptation, and FA position reconfiguration provides micro-mobility for fine-grained local channel adaptation. We then identify key design challenges arising from the high computational complexity and information uncertainty in the joint optimization of UAV trajectory, FA positions, and beamforming under mobility, energy, hardware, and security constraints. To address these challenges, we present a hybrid offline-online optimization framework, where prior information such as environment maps, user mobility patterns, and statistical channel state information (CSI) supports long-term planning, while real-time information such as instantaneous CSI and UAV state enables fast online correction. A case study on FA-enabled UAV-assisted vehicular communications demonstrates the effectiveness of the proposed framework. Finally, we discuss future directions of FA-enabled UAV communication in integrated sensing and communication (ISAC), secure communication, mobile edge computing (MEC), and aerial relaying.

物理与数据驱动的复合扰动分离预测控制：多约束机载光电吊舱

  • 作者Yaokun Lu, Yifan Li, Zhixuan Zhang, Enmei Wang, Hao Teng, Jianzhong Qiao
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-26T05:06:52Z

  • PDF 链接

**研究背景：**
机载光电吊舱在载机振动、输入延时与执行器饱和等多重约束下，需要同时实现视轴稳定与目标跟踪。周期性振动与非周期性扰动混杂，使传统单一扰动观测与补偿难以兼顾：线性自抗扰类方法往往把异质扰动合并估计，在频率变化和参数失配时容易放大误差。论文据此提出按物理特性分离扰动、并分别嵌入优化与补偿通道的约束指向控制方案，以提升复杂工况下的指向精度。

**研究思路：**
作者将复合扰动拆为周期与非周期两部分：自适应周期观测器用线性回归做频率估计，预测多谐波振动并在 MPC 时域内前馈；线性观测器估计非周期扰动，并用有界 LSTM 预测其在输入延时内的变化。周期预测进入 MPC 模型、非周期补偿置于优化器之外，从而抑制非周期估计误差放大。在给定余量包络下用输入集收紧保证指令可行，并给出区域实用跟踪界。通过自建无人机两轴吊舱室内悬停对比实验，以及频率变化与参数失配仿真验证，偏航/俯仰 RMSE 达 0.770°/0.350°，较线性自抗扰分别降低 25.1% 与 45.9%。

**局限性：**
摘要显示验证以室内悬停实验和仿真为主，未说明室外飞行、大机动或强振动工况下的表现；LSTM 的有界性与跟踪界依赖输入序列与预测状态裕度等显式条件，实际满足程度未知；频率估计与参数失配的鲁棒范围、算力实时性摘要未提供足够信息判断。

**值得关注：**
“周期进优化器、非周期出优化器”的扰动分离架构，为具身平台中多源扰动补偿提供了可复用范式；结合物理观测器与有界学习模块预测延时内扰动的思路，以及用输入集收紧换取可行性的约束处理方式，对无人机云台、机械臂视觉伺服等指向控制任务具有借鉴价值。

**研究思路：**

**局限性：**
摘要未说明具体实验平台、场景规模与真实飞行验证情况，估计器的置信界依赖采样分布等统计假设，其失效边界仅被笼统提及；在强湍流、多源或传感器漂移等条件下的表现，摘要中未提供足够信息判断。

**值得关注：**
把经典统计估计引入机器人感知决策，仅用常数级内存实现可证明的停止准则，对资源受限的具身平台气味寻源、泄漏检测与无人机自主收尾具有借鉴意义，也提示“何时停止”应作为导航策略的一等公民来设计。

HARP: Handoff-Aware Recovery Planning for Active Target Acquisition in Multi-UAV Handoff

  • 作者Heegyeong Kim, Alice James, Avishkar Seth, Endrowednes Kuantama, Richard Han
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-24T12:47:38Z
  • 分类cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Continuous aerial tracking often requires multiple drones to maintain target coverage over long periods. During handoff, the receiving UAV may lack prior target observations and must actively acquire a usable view without disrupting the sender's ongoing coverage. This work introduces HARP, a handoff-aware recovery planner that registers bounded sender predictions in the receiver's local map, distinguishes field-of-view failure from physical occlusion, and generates failure-specific yaw, translation, and parallax viewpoints. Candidate motions are checked for receiver clearance, sender-fiducial observability, protected sender--target visibility, temporal validity, and deadline compliance. When a complete trajectory cannot be admitted, HARP may execute a sampled admissible nonzero prefix and replan from updated evidence. In simulation, HARP acquired the target in all 49 standard trials and in 15 of 16 stress trials. Field trials validated HARP's recovery geometry under field-of-view limitation and environmental occlusions.

Supervising a multi-UAV reconnaissance mission usually means plotting waypoints and watching every drone. We present UAV SwarmAgent, a hierarchical multi-agent system in which an operator states the mission in one natural-language sentence and a ground-station agent writes and executes code that turns it into per-UAV routes. The agent grounds inspection targets in an aerial map with a vision-language model, splits them among N UAVs by balancing route length, and dispatches worker agents that verify incidents with a compact onboard decision model. In simulation over 4 incident types and 3 foundation models, the swarm located the incident in 244 of 255 missions (95.7%). We discuss what this predictability, and the false alarms we observed, imply for an operator who supervises the swarm, and outline the user study needed to test it.

Practical Complete Tracking Through Gimbaled Unidirectional Actuation with Application to Multirotor UAVs

  • 作者Ian Willebeek-LeMair, Craig Woolsey
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-22T22:11:44Z
  • 分类eess.SY
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

This paper addresses the problem of practical tracking control for robotic vehicles with gimbaled unidirectional actuators, with special application to multirotor unmanned aerial vehicles. A widely recognized obstacle to tracking control for multirotors is the free-fall singularity --- when a multirotor is commanded to free fall, the vehicle's target attitude becomes undefined. This singularity is a pathology pertinent to all robotic vehicles with gimbaled unidirectional actuators. This paper considers the general theoretical problem for vehicles with gimbaled actuators. Starting from a baseline robust controller that assumes unconstrained inputs, we redesign the control law to be compatible with the gimbaled actuator. This is accomplished by driving the output of the gimbaled actuator to a ball centered at the target input value. The baseline controller's tracking guarantees are recovered in a practical sense. We apply our results to synthesize a global practical complete position tracking controller for multirotor unmanned aerial vehicles. The theory is demonstrated in simulation.
