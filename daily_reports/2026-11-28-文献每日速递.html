Reference-Model-Guided Distributed MPC for UAV Swarm Formation Flying Under Switching Directed Graphs

  • 作者Yiming Wang, Yenan Wu, Junda Wu, Yanke Sun, Huizhe Li, Ke Yu, Youmin Gong, Guangfu Ma, Jie Mei
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-25T15:21:09Z
  • 分类cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Distributed formation control of unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) swarms in complex environments faces several challenges, including switching inter-agent connectivity, limited communication, external disturbances, and collision avoidance. To address these challenges, we propose a reference-model-guided distributed model predictive control (MPC) framework for UAV swarm formation. Each UAV uses relative-position information from its neighbors as input to a local reference model, whose output serves as the formation guidance for the onboard MPC controller. Meanwhile, a Kalman filter is utilized to predict neighboring UAV motions, enabling the construction of inter-UAV collision-avoidance constraints solely from instantaneous relative-state information. By decoupling MPC optimization from distributed coordination, this control architecture preserves the look-ahead planning and constraint-handling capabilities of MPC while enhancing the swarm's adaptability to switching directed graphs. Moreover, eliminating the exchange of neighbors' predicted trajectories reduces communication bandwidth demands, offering strong scalability for large-scale UAV swarms. To improve robustness and safety, disturbance-observer-based feedforward compensation is employed, while the bounded residual disturbance is explicitly considered in the control barrier function (CBF)-based obstacle-avoidance constraints. Finally, the proposed framework is validated through both simulations and real-world flight experiments. Specifically, the flight verification is conducted on a fully distributed multi-UAV platform without external global localization.

A Simple Yet Effective Agent for Open-World Aerial Object Search

  • 作者Jitai Hao, Liangxing Yang, Zhenyang Li, Fan Liu, Qiang Huang, Jun Yu
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-25T10:17:35Z
  • 分类cs.CV, cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Open-world aerial object search methods often rely on persistent spatial maps, learned world models, or task-specific policy training. To examine how far a minimal, training-free design can go, we introduce SimpleUAV, an agent built around a frozen, off-the-shelf vision-language model (VLM). SimpleUAV separates current-view target assessment from history-conditioned action selection, combining fresh candidate evidence with lightweight search context. Local geometry converts visual selections into bounded straight-line movements, depth-based checks halt blocked segments, and measured execution feedback guides subsequent decisions. This closed loop sustains search without persistent spatial maps or task-specific training. SimpleUAV achieves 41.61% Success Rate (SR) on UAV-ON and 11.98% on AerialDojo-200K, compared with 23.80% and 3.20% for the strongest prior baselines, respectively. With limited benchmark-specific adaptation, the same core agent transfers to aerial Vision-and-Language Navigation (VLN) on TravelUAV, improving Full SR by at least 19.66% across all three splits under L1 assistance. These results establish a strong baseline for evaluating when additional architectural complexity benefits aerial search.

A Global Solver for Minimum-Time Connectivity Restoration in UAV Networks

  • 作者Huan Lin, Lianghui Ding
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-25T06:30:55Z
  • 分类cs.NI
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Node failures can split an unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) network into disconnected groups. Restoring communication requires moving the surviving aircraft, and the operator needs both an executable movement plan and an assessment of how much shorter the recovery could be. We develop Connectivity Restoration Branch-and-Bound (CR-BB), which returns a feasible plan and numerical displacement bounds; dividing these bounds by the common speed gives the corresponding time interval. Exact-arithmetic guarantees are established by the theorems. When the gap is not closed, the plan is feasible but not certified optimal. A fixed-tree subproblem is a second-order cone program. CR-BB searches over spanning trees and uses a Joint Mandatory-Link Bound (JMLB) to remove tree families that cannot improve the incumbent; Progress-based Adaptive Stopping (PAS) controls the search budget. On 200 matched cases, enabling the JMLB in the branch bound raised the number of cases meeting the prescribed displacement gap from 182 to 200 and reduced mean expanded nodes from 49.6 to 0.7. On a 60-case exact comparison, CR-BB met the gap in 90.0% of cases, compared with 55.0% for a spanning-tree mixed-integer conic formulation and 37.5% of the eight runs completed by an independent optimizer. On 1,000 paired recovery cases, CR-BB planned first restored connectivity in 72.35 steps on average, compared with 124.32 for an edge-augmentation baseline with conic refinement, a 41.8% reduction; on the common 50% subset the reduction was 28.1%. Across 2,466 cases, PAS reduced cumulative solver time by 20.9% and expanded nodes by 31.1% relative to a fixed search budget, with a 0.36-step mean increase in planned steps; most of the saving came from cases where neither configuration met the gap, and 60 plans became longer.

GOLD-SLAM: Gaussian-splatting Omnidirectional LiDAR-Depth SLAM

  • 作者Weicong Zhang, Yuan Huang, Yu Zheng, Lin Chenxiao, Yingying Li, Lilong Fan, Liangdong Zhuang, Yufan Chen, Ruiping Liu, Junwei Zheng, Jiaming Zhang
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-24T10:20:41Z
  • 分类cs.CV, cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Panoramic cameras capture nearly the entire spherical field of view in a single frame. However, the mismatch between this panoramic coverage and the field of view of LiDAR poses a substantial challenge for panoramic Gaussian splatting SLAM that relies on LiDAR depth priors. We introduce GOLD-SLAM, a Gaussian-splatting Omnidirectional LiDAR-Depth SLAM system that combines high-quality rendering with accurate geometric reconstruction. It performs tracking and mapping in the spherical domain and uses tracked visual features in regions without LiDAR coverage to improve pose estimation. To address insufficient coverage of newly observed surfaces and floating artifacts during incremental mapping, we introduce Depth-Aware Map Extension and Pruning (DAEP). To enable broader evaluation, we collect a new dataset, WHOLE, using a UAV platform across Weak-geometry, High-dynamic, Omnidirectional, Low-light, and Easy scenarios. Extensive experiments on public benchmarks and WHOLE demonstrate competitive rendering quality and improved geometric accuracy relative to perspective systems. Compared with existing panoramic baselines, GOLD-SLAM improves mean PSNR by 5.56-9.10 dB and reduces mean Depth-L1 by 71.9-83.9%. To assess deployment feasibility on resource-constrained platforms such as UAVs, we evaluate an embedded configuration of GOLD-SLAM on a Jetson Orin NX (16 GB), demonstrating online operation at the cost of reconstruction quality.
