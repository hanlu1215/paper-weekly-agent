Cooperative Aerial Transportation of a Payload with a Sliding Cable Attachment

  • 作者Sebastian Tilaguy Lezama, Diego S D'Antonio
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-09T14:35:25Z
  • 分类cs.RO, cs.MA
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

This paper presents a dual-quadrotor hook- suspended transport system for cooperative aerial manipulation of a payload that is not rigidly attached to a fixed point along the cable. Two quadrotors are connected by a single inextensible cable that passes through a frictionless hook mounted on the payload, allowing the cable to slide between its two segments. This introduces a cable-distribution variable that couples the quadrotor motion, cable geometry, and payload dynamics. A feasible reference-generation framework is formulated using the desired payload pose, cable distribution, and system shape while satisfying the cable-length constraint and elliptical workspace. The quadrotors track the geometrically generated trajectories while maintaining positive cable tension and respecting actuator limits. The payload pose is estimated from the system geometry, and its measured position is used only to validate the desired trajectory. The approach is evaluated through experimental demonstrations.

Direct Vector Field Guidance for Path Following

  • 作者Amit Shivam, Nikhil Kumar Singh, Luís Tiago Paiva, Fernando A. C. C. Fontes
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-09T13:28:25Z
  • 分类eess.SY
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

This paper presents a path-following guidance framework for a turn-rate-constrained unmanned aerial vehicle (UAVs). The proposed guidance strategy uses commanded course angle as a tanh-hyperbolic shaping function of the lateral deviation from the desired path. Stability characteristics illustrating asymptotic convergence to the desired path are established using Lyapunov analysis. Analytical expressions for the turn-rate and corresponding maximum turn-rate bound are derived, enabling direct gain selection for a turn-rate-limited vehicle. Numerical simulations are performed for straight-line following scenarios under both ideal and non-ideal initial heading conditions. Furthermore, a comparative study against representative arctan-, arcsine-, and arccos-based vector-field guidance laws is conducted using analytically feasible gains selected from their respective turn-rate bounds. Comparative numerical results demonstrate the proposed formulation achieves reliable boundary convergence while maintaining smooth steering behavior and a compact analytical gain-design framework.

Multi-Modal Graph Neural Network-Inspired Vessel Identification and Tracking for UAV-Based Maritime Surveillance

  • 作者Chenjie Zhao, Jiangtian Nie, Jiawen Kang, Yuxu Lu, Ryan Wen Liu
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-09T08:37:39Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

The unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV)-based maritime surveillance is becoming an important part of maritime Internet of Things (IoT) and intelligent transportation systems. It provides a flexible and cost-effective way to large-scale maritime traffic monitoring. However, achieving robust situational awareness remains challenging in practical maritime environments. The main challenges arise from heterogeneous sensing modalities, adverse imaging conditions, and spatial misalignment caused by UAV motion. To address these problems, this paper proposes an airborne multi-modal graph learning-driven framework, termed AMGT, for vessel identification and tracking in UAV-assisted maritime IoT. Specifically, visible and infrared images are first aligned and fused through a multi-parameter perspective transformation model. This improves visual perception under low visibility and complex illumination conditions. Automatic identification system (AIS) messages are then temporally synchronized using interpolation and linear prediction. To detect small and oriented vessels, we develop a mixed-modality MAir-YOLOv8-OBB detector, followed by the MAir-SORT algorithm for stable vessel tracking. We further design a graph learning-driven association network to model temporal dynamics and spatial dependencies among vessels and between vessels and UAVs. A motion-aware coordinate unification (MCU) strategy is also introduced to reduce nonlinear misalignment caused by UAV motion. Experiments on the MAP-M2UAV50 dataset show that AMGT achieves high accuracy and efficiency. The results demonstrate its potential for UAV-assisted maritime IoT applications, including vessel monitoring, traffic situational awareness, and intelligent maritime transportation services. The dataset and code will be available at https://github.com/MAPGroup/AMGT.

Prediction-Informed Landing Window Planning for UAV Landing on USV in High Sea States

  • 作者Kaizhen Li, Xi Zhou, Dan Zhang, Xiaowei Li
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-09T08:12:13Z
  • 分类cs.RO, cs.LG
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Autonomous landing of unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) on unmanned surface vehicle (USV) is difficult in high sea states. Existing hierarchical landing methods often infer future USV motion only implicitly from short observation histories, which limits their ability to adapt to severe and rapidly changing sea conditions. This paper presents a prediction-informed hierarchical control framework. A pretrained model is developed to explicitly predict the USV attitude during high-level landing decisions. The prediction helps identify suitable landing windows and choose the right touchdown time, while a conventional low-level flight controller maintains UAV stability and tracks motion commands. To evaluate the framework under realistic disturbances, a real-sim validation setup is constructed in which USV-motion data collected in real sea conditions are transferred to a MuJoCo USV. The experiments demonstrate the feasibility of using explicit attitude prediction to improve landing-window selection and support timing-aware UAV landing planning in rapidly varying marine environments.

Measurable Semantic 3D Gaussian Splatting with Joint Region and Depth Supervision for Interactive Building Inspection

  • 作者Yi Zhang, Tian Tan, Ray Kai Leung Su
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-09T07:33:37Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Image-based 3D reconstruction provides a scalable representation for building inspection and maintenance. However, photorealistic reconstruction alone does not fully provide the geometric measurability, semantic structure, and interactive information access required for engineering applications. This study proposes a measurable semantic 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) framework with region and depth supervision for interactive building inspection from multi-view imagery. Mask-based region supervision suppresses irrelevant backgrounds and focuses optimization on target regions. Point-wise depth supervision guides Gaussian centers toward physical surfaces using reference geometry derived from multi-view stereo (MVS) point clouds, providing geometric anchors for measuring physical dimensions and areas. A semantic Gaussian field associates learnable class features with individual Gaussian primitives, enabling semantic rendering and component-level inspection. These capabilities are operationalized through AEC-SplatTwin, an interactive building inspection platform supporting real-time visualization, semantic analysis, quantitative measurement, and multimodal large language model (MLLM)-assisted interaction. On the synthetic UAV-inspection QuakeCity and smartphone-captured HKUPB datasets, the framework reduces Gaussian-center depth MAE by 58.1% and 86.8%, while its semantic field achieves mIoU values of 0.8529 and 0.8642. On HKUPB, the mean relative errors for height, width, and area measurements are 0.33%, 1.07%, and 0.60%. These results show that the proposed framework extends 3DGS beyond visual reconstruction into a measurable, semantically structured, and interactive engineering representation for building inspection.

Structure-Aware Matching for Robust UAV Thermal-to-Satellite Geo-Localization

  • 作者Chun Liu, Yuxin Wang, Zhenxiang Huo, Linlin Zhu, Tao Xu, Henggang Han
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-09T00:38:22Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

UAV thermal-to-satellite geo-localization localizes UAVs by matching real-time thermal images against georeferenced satellite images, enabling autonomous navigation in GNSS-denied and low-visibility conditions. However, substantial cross-modal disparities in color, brightness and viewpoint hinder conventional appearance-based methods from establishing reliable correspondences. While existing approaches predominantly address global modality shifts, they overlook the invariant scene structures inherently shared between thermal and satellite views. To bridge this gap, we propose SAMF, a Structure-Aware Matching Framework that explicitly exploits stable cross-modal structural cues to improve UAV thermal geolocalization. Specifically, the Directional Structure Enhancement Module (DSEM) strengthens robust structural representations by capturing directional and low-frequency spatial patterns, and the Modality-Aware Affine Remapping Module (MARM) subsequently applies affine correction to these enhanced features, further promoting cross-modal alignment. Additionally, we introduce the Thermal-UAV-Paired benchmark derived from the Thermal-UAV dataset. Extensive experiments on both the Boson-nighttime and Thermal-UAV-Paired datasets show that SAMF achieves state-of-the-art performance. Ablation studies further validate the effectiveness and synergistic complementarity of DSEM and MARM. The code and dataset are released at: https://github.com/YuxinWang879/SAMF.git.

Verifier-Mediated Multi-LLM Coordination for Multi-Robot Path Planning

  • 作者Vivek Khatana, Zijian Song, Naira Hovakimyan, Petros Voulgaris
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-08T22:12:10Z
  • 分类cs.RO, eess.SY
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Foundation models are increasingly used for high-level multi-robot task planning, yet independently generated symbolic plans can become jointly infeasible after geometric grounding because of shared spatial and temporal constraints. We study execution-aware coordination of large-language-model (LLM) planners for multi-robot navigation. We compare a centralized architecture, in which one LLM jointly reasons over the robot team, with a decentralized multi-LLM architecture in which each robot independently proposes a task plan. A geometric verifier grounds the candidate plans into robot trajectories, detects predicted inter-robot conflicts, and converts conflict information into structured feedback for targeted replanning. This verifier-mediated architecture separates semantic task reasoning from execution-level feasibility while enabling iterative coordination among LLM planners. Experiments in CARLA and on Crazyflie aerial robots evaluate coordination performance as task complexity increases and characterize the trade-offs between centralized reasoning, decentralized verifier-mediated coordination, and physical executability.

Robust and Scalable UAV-UGV Planning for Aerial Monitoring under Energy and Precedence Constraints

  • 作者Cahit Ikbal Er, Yasin Yazicioglu
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-08T19:47:55Z
  • 分类cs.RO, cs.MA, eess.SY
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

We consider planning for unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) and unmanned ground vehicle (UGV) teams in aerial monitoring missions with ordering requirements on the monitoring points to be visited by the UAV. The system consists of multiple UAV-UGV teams, each comprising one UAV and one UGV. UAV energy limitations are modeled through endurance, defined as the maximum flight time on a full battery. The UGVs serve as mobile charging stations and transport the UAVs between consecutive aerial tours. Given a set of monitoring points subject to ordering constraints and the teams' initial and terminal positions, the objective is to minimize mission completion time while satisfying UAV energy limits and the required visit order under bounded travel-time disturbances that may arise during execution. Unlike prior work, the plan jointly determines vehicle routes and their timing, allowing teams to slow down as needed to satisfy the ordering constraints. We develop a polynomial-time algorithm that generates efficient solutions to the underlying NP-hard planning problem. We establish feasibility guarantees, characterize the algorithm's computational complexity, and evaluate its performance via numerical comparisons.

Fly-by-Code: Embodied Coding Agents for Aerial Manipulation with Active Visual and Physical Feedback

  • 作者Jaewoo Lee, Jeongyeon Seo, Sihyun Cho, Gyeongrak Choe, Yutong Wang, Bavin Saravanan, Jia-Bin Huang, Furong Huang, Sebastian Scherer, Guanya Shi, H. Jin Kim, Seungjae Lee, Dongjae Lee
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-08T18:00:00Z
  • 分类cs.RO, cs.CV, cs.LG
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Embodied coding agents construct robot policies from natural-language instructions by generating executable programs and refining them from execution feedback. This refinement assumes that execution feedback reveals whether the task succeeded and, after a failure, provides sufficient evidence to guide program revision. Aerial manipulation violates this assumption in two ways. Payload constraints restrict onboard camera coverage and leave task-critical outcomes outside the robot's view (partial observability), while strong dynamic coupling and limited control margins make physical interactions fragile (limited interaction robustness) and leave failure causes unresolved. Re-examining the same feedback cannot recover observations that were never collected or separate failure causes that the available evidence does not distinguish. To acquire this missing evidence, we introduce Active Feedback (AF), an active "View and Probe" framework. When execution feedback leaves unresolved questions, the agent synthesizes a separate diagnostic program that runs in simulation to inspect unseen outcomes from informative viewpoints (View) or test hypotheses about failure causes through targeted physical interactions (Probe). Across six simulated aerial manipulation tasks with 60 trials per condition, AF reduces false completion declarations from 25 to 5 and raises final task success from 31.7% to 75% compared with terminal feedback under the same coding-agent configuration and task-execution limit. Using shared robot APIs, task programs refined with AF in simulation run on a physical aerial manipulator without task-program source-code modification and complete multi-stage manipulation tasks in the real world. These results show that executable diagnosis fills gaps in execution feedback for embodied coding agents, improving both completion assessment and program refinement in aerial manipulation.
