**研究背景：**
水上物联网在海事搜救等场景中需要计算支持与覆盖。无人机可提供低成本、灵活的空中服务，地面站可提供强算力支持，二者协同有望服务复杂场景中的水上物联网。但该协同面临任务不确定性、轨迹不确定性、异构性和计算资源受限等挑战，现有协作方式仍难以充分应对。论文因此研究无人机与地面站协同的鲁棒MEC，并通过联合任务卸载与轨迹优化降低总执行时间。

**研究思路：**
作者将联合任务卸载与无人机轨迹建模为最小化总执行时间的混合整数非线性规划问题，该问题NP难。为此提出生成式AI增强的异构智能体近端策略优化算法GAI-HAPPO：利用生成式AI增强actor网络对复杂环境的建模与高层特征提取，以预测不确定性并适应动态条件；同时稳定critic网络，缓解多智能体强化学习的不稳定性。仿真结果表明，该算法优于现有基准方法，展示了处理复杂跨域问题的潜力。

**局限性：**
摘要仅说明进行了广泛仿真，未提供真实水上系统验证、数据规模、不确定性建模假设及GAI模型依赖等信息；能耗、可扩展性和安全隐私等也未见说明。因此，摘要中未提供足够信息判断具体局限性，实际部署有效性仍需验证。

**值得关注：**
该工作将生成式AI与异构多智能体强化学习结合，用于无人机—地面站协同MEC中的任务卸载和轨迹决策，强调动态不确定性下的预测与稳定训练。其方法思路可为具身智能系统在通信—计算—移动耦合场景中的协同决策、跨域资源调度及仿真评价设计提供参考。

Reliable Tree Recruitment from Scarce Annotations for Forest Inventory Expansion

  • 作者Luiz Fernando Souza Luz, Pedro J. Soto Vega, Matheus Pinheiro Ferreira, Laura Elena Cué la Rosa, Hudson Franklin Pessoa Veras, Ernandes Macedo da Cunha Neto, Evandro Orfanó Figueiredo, Ana Paula Dalla Corte, Carlos Roberto Sanquetta, José Matheus Segre Moneva Viveiros, Matheus Santos Fuza, Heitor Eduardo Ferreira Campos Morato Filpi, Cristiano Vinícius Vidal, Vinícius Moura Costa, Débora Nascimento Rosário, Danilo Roberti Alves de Almeida, Paulo Guilherme Molin, Dário Augusto Borges Oliveira
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-18T13:31:37Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Species-level inventories of individual tree crowns (ITCs) from UAV imagery are limited by annotation cost: in tropical forests, many species are represented by only a handful of field-validated crowns. We propose a reliable tree recruitment framework that expands a small annotated inventory by iteratively self-training a multi-task DeepLabV3 network, combining semantic segmentation with a crown-distance regression head, on RGB UAV orthomosaics. At each iteration, candidate crowns enter the training inventory only if they pass a confidence filter on class probability and distance map and a morphology filter on crown area and solidity, with at most five new crowns per species, keeping recruited samples consistent with the reference inventory. We evaluate the framework at two Brazilian sites: an Amazonian rainforest in Acre (17 species, 355 annotated crowns, 4 cm GSD) and a forest site in the Cerrado of Maranhão (4 species, 51 annotated crowns, 1.8 cm GSD). Over 20 iterations, the number of retained crowns grew from about 500 to 960 in Acre and from about 700 to over 3100 in Maranhão, while crown-level F1 exceeded single- and multi-task DeepLabV3 baselines (53.5% vs. 50.0% and 45.2%; 92.2% vs. 79.7% and 83.6%). The framework offers a low-cost route to expanding species inventories with consumer-grade UAVs and limited fieldwork.

CommDreamer: Radio-Geometric World Models for Mapless Cellular-Connected UAV Trajectory Planning

  • 作者Yuqi Ping, Tianhao Liang, Tingting Zhang
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-18T11:22:40Z
  • 分类cs.RO, eess.SY
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Reliable cellular connectivity and collision-free flight are essential for unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) operations in low-altitude urban canyons, where dense 3D buildings obstruct flight clearance, shadow serving base stations, and create strong co-channel interference at street intersections. Existing planners either require prior 3D city meshes and channel knowledge maps unavailable onboard, or rely on model-free reinforcement learning with low sample efficiency and poor cross-city generalization. We propose CommDreamer, a mapless radio-geometric world model that fuses raw onboard 3D LiDAR scans, multicell cellular reports, and shift-invariant relative kinematics to jointly infer local obstacle clearance and directional signal-to-interference-plus-noise ratio (SINR) dynamics. Using a categorical recurrent state-space model, CommDreamer compresses multimodal measurement histories into a coordinate-invariant latent belief, enabling the UAV to foresee deep shadow zones and train a lightweight online policy purely via multi-step latent imagination without prior maps or antenna patterns. Zero-shot evaluations across unseen urban morphologies in our open-source ray-tracing simulator, AeroCity-RT, show that CommDreamer converges within 150 episodes where model-free reinforcement learning fails to complete flights, achieving 100.0% mission success with zero collisions while lifting median route SINR to 8.02 dB and cutting effective SINR outage by over 50% relative to radio-blind world-model and reactive LiDAR baselines.

AeroRSI: Recursive Self-Improvement for Aerial Vision-Language Navigation

  • 作者Shiwei Wu, Yuwen Pan, Zhi Li, Yaowen Li, Yan Zhou, Xuemiao Xu, Yu Liu
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-18T09:07:04Z
  • 分类cs.CV, cs.AI, cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Vision-language-action (VLA) models enable unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) to follow natural-language instructions, yet still fail frequently in closed-loop flight. Correcting these failures requires repairs, i.e., actions that avoid them at the states the policy itself reaches, which human pilots cannot label at scale and rule-based planners cannot supply reliably. In this paper, we present AeroRSI, a recursive self-improvement framework in which an aerial navigation policy iteratively learns from its own failures. Our key insight is that a failing policy should judge its own repairs by flying on from them. Judged in this way, its sampled actions and even rule-based proposals that hurt it when imitated directly become reliable supervision. Moreover, an episode terminates at its first failure, concealing subsequent failures until the earlier ones are repaired. AeroRSI thus renews its supervision in every round, anchoring the failures of the current policy, verifying repairs by its continuation, and distilling them while keeping its behavior elsewhere. On TravelUAV, three rounds raise the success rate of a supervised 2B-parameter policy from 46.4% to 67.5% and reduce collisions by half, surpassing the previous state of the art by 10.6 points from the same initialization without extra human labels or inference cost. Code and models are available at https://github.com/starsholic/AeroRSI.

A Hierarchical Onboard Vision-Language-Navigation Framework for Aerial Robots in Complex Environments

  • 作者Yuze Wu, Mo Zhu, Xingxing Li, Yuheng Du, Zhaoqi Wang, Yuxin Fan, Wenjun Li, Kenghou Hoi, Zhichao Han, Xin Zhou, Fei Gao
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-18T06:21:08Z
  • 分类cs.RO, cs.AI
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) are expected to achieve semantic reasoning, intelligent decision-making, and long-horizon navigation under strict onboard constraints. While recent foundation-model-driven approaches have shown promising progress toward this goal, existing methods remain limited by challenges in 3D data availability, trajectory feasibility, long-horizon reasoning, and computational efficiency in complex environments. To bridge these gaps, we propose VLN-AR, a hierarchical vision-language-navigation (VLN) framework for aerial robots. VLN-AR decouples multimodal reasoning and low-level control, enabling structured prediction of 3D pose commands and navigation intentions that are translated into dynamically feasible actions. To mitigate data scarcity and domain gaps, we introduce a fully automated 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS)-based pipeline to generate scalable photorealistic UAV navigation data. A progressive three-stage training strategy further enhances grounding, scene reasoning, and complex navigation. In addition, a lightweight cognitive agent improves long-horizon reasoning via memory-aware task decomposition. Finally, we design onboard optimization techniques, including token sparsification and model lightweighting, achieving 7.1x-9.4x speedup while maintaining high navigation performance on resource-limited UAV platforms. Experiments show great improvements over existing methods in both simulation and real-world UAV navigation. Project website: https://wyz000.github.io/VLN-AR/

GRID-UAV: Geometry and Ray-depth Integrated Decision-making for Unmanned Aerial Vehicles

  • 作者Anas Anwarul Haq Khan, Amrin Kareem, Muhammad Umer Sheikh, Biplab Banerjee, Muhammad Haris Khan
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-17T20:41:30Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Vision-language-action models (VLAs) encode images with 2D encoders that capture little 3D structure. A common approach is to feed the policy estimated depth, but a UAV chooses its own altitude, must judge distances tens of meters away, where monocular depth is less reliable, and must relate its forward and downward views. On long aerial routes, adding depth features directly brings no significant gain (42.5% vs. 42.6% success), so we study how 3D information should be given to the policy. Analyzing flight failures, we find that two forms of 3D grounding fail in opposite ways: explicit 3D lifting of depth collides less on the way but more often fails to finish at the target, while implicit alignment with a 3D geometry model finishes more often but collides more. We propose GRID, which combines both: (1) it lifts monocular depth into ray-corrected 3D positions in the UAV's frame for both cameras, and (2) during training only, it aligns its visual features with a frozen multi-view geometry model, at no inference cost. GRID adding explicit lifting to implicit alignment significantly increases how often the policy reaches the target ($61.5\% \rightarrow 69.5\%$, $p<0.001$), and adding implicit alignment to explicit lifting significantly increases how often it completes the task once there ($57.7\% \rightarrow 61.4\%$, $p<0.05$). On TravelUAV (3,005 test episodes), GRID achieves the best success rate on every split, with relative gains over the state of the art of 9.5% (seen), 17.1% (unseen objects) and 25.4% (unseen map).

**研究背景：**
海事搜救需要快速定位落水人员，无人机巡查因此具有应用需求。但飞行中的相机运动和低光下较长曝光会模糊小目标，妨碍人员检测。事件相机记录亮度变化，可减轻长曝光带来的运动模糊，论文据此研究其用于海事人员检测的可行性。

**研究思路：**

**值得关注：**

受守护集群在环：UAV/UGV协同的ROS 2/PX4数字孪生

  • 作者Uwe M. Borghoff, Paolo Bottoni, Luca D'Aurizio, Remo Pareschi

**研究背景：**
在对抗环境中，UAV/UGV集群的物理安全不能仅依赖软件，因为软件栈及其网络本身就是攻击面。Guarded Swarms 曾提出用TB-CSPN数字协调层配合独立模拟安全层来否决不安全指令，但此前只完成架构规格与论证，尚未执行。论文旨在给出首个可执行实例，检验该架构的安全保证能否在系统中落地。

**研究思路：**
作者用ROS 2、PX4软件在环和Gazebo构建数字孪生，设置两架UAV与一辆UGV的海岸反无人机场景；将TB-CSPN从多模态感知、人工授权到车辆任务分派的迁移链一一映射为ROS 2节点，并把模拟层建模为逐车虚拟安全板，门控每条指令。八组实验、60次反事实运行表明：三条负向保证在链路三个阶段成立；威胁确认受最窄感知模态限制；安全层可独立行使建模包络；故障注入下，独立门控能阻止伪造任务或被攻陷worker导致的碰撞，而将否决权交给被攻陷worker则无效。孪生还暴露了实现级风险，并区分了仿真可验证与仅硬件可验证的性质。

**局限性：**
验证主要停留在数字孪生和软件在环仿真，真实硬件、真实网络攻击与复杂野外环境仍待检验；场景仅两UAV一UGV，规模和任务泛化性有限；安全层的“建模包络”依赖模型假设。摘要未提供足够信息判断统计显著性、基线比较及更多局限性。

**值得关注：**
该工作展示了“数字协调层+独立模拟否决层”的纵深防御思路，以及把形式化协调链映射到ROS 2节点的工程方法。其反事实测试、故障注入和独立门控对比，对多机器人具身系统的安全评价、对抗环境任务授权和仿真—硬件验证边界划分具有借鉴意义。

五层框架下的基准测量审计：以无人机意图识别为例

  • 作者Zhirong Su, Tian Zhang
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-17T09:08:17Z
  • 分类cs.LG, cs.AI
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

**研究背景：**
基准准确率常被直接当作模型“理解”所测构念的证据，但准确率可能来自数据记忆、静态线索或与任务无关的特征，而非目标能力。现有评测多报告单一精度指标，缺少对“观测是否可靠、表征是否可解码、解码结果是否被真正使用、行为是否一致”的分层审查，因而难以判断高分背后的真实机制。论文以无人机意图识别为实例，试图把准确率这一笼统读数拆解为可检验的多层证据链，为基准测量审计提供框架。

**研究思路：**
作者提出观察、表征、解码、使用、行为五层框架，区分编码、可解码性与实际使用，并在 AeroIntent 上实例化。观察层发现 20 个 Drone 程序中有 7 个不可靠，径向速度的直接视觉对应量在 0.26% 尺度变化下触底；表征层仅显示两个任务设计的 RGB 特征有名义信号，RAFT 光流径向偏置 r=+0.257（p=0.030），边界框光流散度 r=+0.203（p=0.029）；多特征读出未超过簇最小可检测效应，最大全局样本外相关仅 r=+0.080。解码层在 Bonferroni 阈值下设 MDE 为 |r|=0.599，而观测值功效为 0.00，并通过对齐/正交注入实验双向验证 MDE 条件。使用层发现准确率与程序记忆及多区域静态线索一致；行为层显示准确率不依赖径向速度读出（外观 0.6552 对边界框轨迹 0.4080，簇级配对 Δ=+0.2471，p=0.0132）。

**局限性：**
摘要仅给出单一基准 AeroIntent 与无人机意图识别实例，框架能否迁移到其他任务、模态与真实飞行系统尚不明确；观察层可靠性依赖 Drone 程序与仿真尺度设定，注入实验为模拟验证，缺少真实部署检验；各层结论以相关性和效应量为主，因果机制仍有限。摘要未提供足够信息判断具体局限性。

**值得关注：**
该工作提示具身智能与机器人评测不应止于准确率，而应审计“感知—表征—解码—使用—行为”全链路，尤其警惕程序记忆和静态线索造成的虚高。其 MDE、功效分析与注入实验可借鉴到自动驾驶和无人机基准设计，推动建立可解码性与行为一致性并重的评价规范。
