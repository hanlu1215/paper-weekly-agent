Federated Learning for Unmanned Aerial Systems: A Screened Bibliometric Census of an Emergent Sub-Literature

  • 作者Ameen Ur Rehman, Ali Nasir
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-19T15:02:49Z
  • 分类eess.SY
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

We census the literature at the intersection of federated learning (FL) and unmanned aerial systems (UAS) with a broad Scopus query that combines FL, UAV, and delivery/logistics terms (Computer Science and Engineering, 2022--2026). The export holds \textbf{14,965 records}, but only \textbf{7.9\%} of the 14,945 unique records mention both an FL term and a UAV term in title, abstract, or author keywords. That screen leaves \textbf{1,185 records}: 90 in 2022, 361 in 2025, and 332 already indexed for 2026. The screened share of each year's raw output stays near 7.5\%, so the growth is not an artefact of the screen. Term-definition, document-type, and complete-year checks leave the leading venue (\emph{IEEE Internet of Things Journal}, 111 papers, $h{=}30$) and the leading country (China, 628 affiliation instances) unchanged. A manual read of 30 records finds 14 of 26 full papers (54\%, 95\% CI 35--71\%) substantively about FL for UAVs, so the corpus counts keyword co-occurrence, not only focused work. Counting authors by Scopus ID gives 4,095 authors with Lotka exponent $n{=}3.15$ ($R^2{=}0.996$); name strings would merge distinct people and understate the author count by 31\%. An OpenAlex export under the same screen yields 147 records, 74\% of them in the Scopus corpus, but the two exports are not matched populations, so we do not read the volume gap as coverage alone. Finally, of 228 records with a delivery-adjacent term, only 5 (2.2\%) mention \emph{parcel}, \emph{last-mile}, or \emph{courier} (14, or 6.1\%, if \emph{package} counts); the rest reflect transportation, traffic, and generic-logistics idiom. Keyword screening alone therefore cannot isolate last-mile drone-delivery research from the surrounding UAV-networking literature.

无人机集群保持队形GNSS欺骗的伪距与多普勒视差检测

  • 作者Minseok Park, Joon Soo Yoo
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-19T12:35:04Z
  • 分类cs.CR, eess.SP
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

**研究背景：**
无人机集群常通过比对报告位置与已知队形来检测GNSS欺骗，但GNSS-WASP等保持队形攻击可由每颗卫星对应的一个数公里外发射器整体平移集群，从而绕过位置级检查。现有队形一致性方法因此难以充分识别此类攻击。论文旨在利用位置层以下的信号痕迹，解决保持队形欺骗的检测问题，对集群导航安全具有重要意义。

**研究思路：**
作者注意到发射器较近时，各无人机观测同一信号的角度略有差异；这种视差会使信号到达时间在90米集群内产生20—40厘米弯曲，且不受攻击者附加延迟影响。为此提出似然比检验，从无人机已测的伪距和多普勒中提取该痕迹，并在所有时钟与攻击延迟未知、无需额外天线的条件下工作。仿真采用真实GPS/Galileo轨道和白色接收机噪声：16架无人机在5公里GNSS-WASP下，直飞10秒窗口检测率为13%—60%，队形转弯配合精确多普勒接收机时每个窗口均可检测；而原对策最多5%。该检验也能捕获单发射器和地面阵列欺骗。

**局限性：**
摘要显示验证主要基于仿真，真实飞行、多径、接收机噪声建模偏差及复杂卫星几何下的鲁棒性尚不明确；精确多普勒接收机可能是性能条件之一。摘要未提供足够信息判断真实系统验证、不同集群规模与基线影响等具体局限。

**值得关注：**
该工作为集群具身智能安全提供了“跨智能体低层测量一致性”思路：不依赖额外硬件，而利用已有伪距、多普勒中的空间视差和物理约束，并以未知延迟下的统计检验提升鲁棒性。其任务设定、评价窗口和对比基线可供机器人、自动驾驶及GNSS欺骗检测研究借鉴。

AeroBelief: Budgeted First-Action Verification for Evidence-Guided Aerial Sensing

  • 作者Tianren Li, Dongbo Li, Hongkai Chen, Jie Liu, Guoliang Xing
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-19T06:09:28Z
  • 分类cs.AI
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Aerial sensing must combine uncertain overhead context with current observations while budgeting prediction, movement and image processing. AeroBelief uses the next executable action as the unit of verification against reference scores. It groups candidate views by first action, scans them with an inexpensive proposer, and selectively queries a structured world model (WM). Calibrated group bounds support strict commitment; a six-query controller connects selective prediction with belief updates, acceptance checks and scheduled sensing. Across four layout families, 40 worlds and 960 closed-loop executions, the AeroBelief controller completes 75.42% of tasks, compared with 73.33% for exhaustive WM scoring and 65.83% for line-of-sight geometry, using 91.81% fewer reference-candidate evaluations than exhaustive scoring. In fixed-reference replay with a six-query cap, proposer-scaled anchored bounds raised first-action certificate availability from 36.25% to 54.69% and reduced mean queries per state by 13.54% relative to unit-scale anchored bounds. A complete ground-hosted pipeline processes 2,685 self-collected 20-megapixel unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) images from seven DJI Mavic 3M routes. Context-guided admission and feedback retain all eight independently supported target confirmations with 76.05% fewer processed images and 70.02% less accumulated active processing service than uncapped all-frame processing; feedback saves another 5.17% of images relative to static context. The studies connect budgeted action selection with evidence-driven processing of recorded flight imagery.

**研究思路：**

**局限性：**

**值得关注：**
