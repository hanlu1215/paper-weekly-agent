**值得关注：**

净零功耗无人机：多跳中继中的量子HRLLC

  • 作者Ali Ranjha, Georges Kaddoum, Halim Yanikomeroglu
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-16T14:35:36Z
  • 分类eess.SY
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

**研究背景：**
爆炸后化工厂等挑战环境需要超可靠低时延通信（HRLLC），量子使能多跳无人机中继可提供应急覆盖，但严格能量采集约束下，量子资源、二维位置与发射功率高度耦合，形成非凸混合整数非线性规划，传统方法难以高效求解，且需显式考虑退相干、门缺陷和纠缠退化等真实量子物理。

**研究思路：**
作者针对上述耦合优化难题，提出对称双无人机架构与三个顺序量子信道，在能量采集约束下联合优化qubit分配、无人机距离/高度和发射功率，以最小化解码错误；因目标非凸，设计量子资源优化遗传算法QRO-GA求解MINLP。数值仿真表明其优于基准方法，高qubit分配时5次迭代收敛，并揭示80 μs传输窗口内的U型时延权衡，同时优化二维定位并平衡消耗与收集功率，实现净零功耗。

**局限性：**
摘要仅报告数值仿真结果，未说明是否包含真实量子硬件或外场无人机验证；适用场景集中于特定应急环境，模型对量子噪声与能量采集参数的依赖及大规模网络扩展性仍不明确，摘要未提供足够信息判断具体局限性。

**值得关注：**
该工作把量子资源分配、无人机空间部署与功率控制统一为MINLP，并用QRO-GA求解，为能量自治应急中继和具身智能通信系统提供可借鉴的任务建模与评价思路：关注解码错误、收敛速度、严格时延窗口和能量净零平衡。

SkyAgent：面向连续空中搜索的可修正目标记忆

  • 作者Kaibo Yang, Xingyu Wu, Enze Zhu, Yajie Chai, Xuyang Li, Jiahui Cui, Xiaoxuan Liu, Lei Wang, Zhan Chen
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-16T11:00:43Z
  • 分类cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

**研究背景：**
连续空中搜索需要从稀疏、依赖视角的观测中构建目标假设。但若后续证据与已存假设矛盾，该候选仍可能留在规划器中，继续触发接近或验证动作，形成轨迹层面的“假候选持续”（FCP）。摘要指出，已有方法缺少把证据与候选状态绑定并随证据修正的机制，难以处理观测矛盾，本文即针对这一问题展开。

**研究思路：**
针对FCP，作者提出可修正目标记忆（RTM）：将证据关联到持久候选，对相关视角做折扣，并以暂定、确认、拒绝三态修正候选；关键接口把每个生成航点绑定到来源候选，使拒绝即时作废旧目标，同时用保守重激活保护暂时被遮挡目标。在CityAVOS、UAV-ON、OpenUAV的四个划分上，SkyAgent+RTM优于所有对比方法，成功率提升2.90–6.31点，明显降低Behavior-FCP与矛盾后行驶距离，且误拒率低；35个匹配的真实DJI场景中，任务成功率与行为可靠性也稳定提升。

**局限性：**
摘要未说明三态判定阈值、视角折扣的具体设定与计算开销；保守重激活在长时间遮挡或相似目标干扰下是否会引入误检，摘要中未提供足够信息判断。真实实验仅35个匹配场景，规模与环境多样性有限，具体局限仍需全文验证。

**值得关注：**
该工作把“记忆可修正”落实为候选状态机与航点—候选绑定接口，为具身智能体的目标级记忆维护提供了可借鉴设计；FCP作为轨迹级失败指标，补充了仅看成功率的评价方式；三态候选与保守重激活思路可迁移至无人机巡检、搜救及动态目标跟踪等任务。

**研究背景：**
经典随机几何通常预设空间点过程规律与移动模型，侧重正向分析，难以处理非平稳空间网络中由聚合密度演化反演方向性宏观输运的逆问题。该研究由此提出流体时空随机几何（F-STSG）的运动学基础，试图在连续极限下用强度场表示演化移动群体，同时将网络基础设施保留为离散空间测度，从而把宏观输运重构表述为逆边值问题，为信息流与移动场分析提供新框架。

**研究思路：**
作者在强度严格正且有界、源与边界数据相容的条件下，以瞬时加权最小能量准则从可行速度类中选出唯一速度代表，将重构化为加权椭圆Neumann问题，并区分密度可识别的输运与仅凭密度演化不可识别的加权螺线环流；进一步推导信息通量、界面输运、速度—散度关系与质心动力学。验证包括数值实验考察重构一致性和敏感性，pNEUMA数据留出验证内部输运，以及UAV研究评估观察延迟、运动约束和执行扰动下质心作为因果下游控制参考。

**局限性：**
摘要显示方法依赖连续极限、正有界强度、相容源与边界数据及最小能量选择等假设，实际网络中的适用性仍需检验。数值实验和公开数据验证的具体规模、误差与泛化性摘要未说明；UAV研究仅概述控制参考评估，真实系统部署、延迟建模充分性及复杂场景鲁棒性仍待验证。

**值得关注：**
该工作把密度演化的可识别输运与不可识别环流分开，对移动网络、UAV群体和具身系统的信息流反演、可观测性分析与控制设计有启发；质心作为延迟和扰动下的下游控制参考，也值得自动驾驶与机器人集群借鉴。

Integrated Sensing and Communication in 3GPP: Standardization, Performance Evaluation, and Experimental Validation Toward 6G

  • 作者Gosan Noh, Gebremeskel Seiley Abraha, Joshua Yabana, Nathaniel Foli, Michael Ankomah Asare, Evelyn Seyram Kakah, Mildred Nyantakyiwaa Okyere, De-Roy Ekow Sagoe, Shrayan Das, Pekka Kyösti, Junhyeong Kim, Kyoung-Jae Lee, Eunkyung Kim
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-16T06:20:22Z
  • 分类eess.SP
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) is evolving from a primarily physical-layer research into a standardized cellular-network capability spanning channel modeling, radio procedures, network architecture, and the delivery of sensing services. We review this progression within the Third Generation Partnership Project (3GPP), from Release 19 service requirements and target-aware channel modeling to the Release 20 New Radio (NR) sensing evaluation framework, radio access network procedures, and system architecture, and discuss future directions toward 6G. We then present two 3GPP-aligned simulation case studies to demonstrate the corresponding evaluation approaches. The first case study compares hard-decision, soft-decision, and hybrid fusion for unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) sensing using multiple transmission and reception points (TRPs). Detection, false alarms, and conditional position and velocity accuracy are evaluated using receiver-derived angle estimates and separately calibrated method-specific thresholds. The second case study compares ideal and practical sensing-assisted predictive handover with conventional handover, while accounting for sensing error and information age. We further present a testbed-based measurement study that characterizes monostatic and bistatic UAV radar cross sections across frequencies, platforms, and observation angles and provides empirical support for correlated target-response modeling. Finally, we discuss open challenges and future research directions in multi-node fusion, resource and interference management, network evolution, security, privacy, and reproducible evaluation. Together, these components connect calibrated channel models, link- and system-level simulation, real-time prototypes, controlled measurements, and field trials through a staged evaluation roadmap.

UAV Rotatable Antenna under Rotation Delay

  • 作者Yinmei Xie, Chiya Zhang, Chunlong He, Weizheng Zhang, Wei Wang
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-16T00:39:34Z
  • 分类cs.IT, eess.SP
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Rotatable antennas (RAs) can improve wireless link performance by adjusting their boresight directions. However, existing RA studies generally assume instantaneous antenna reconfiguration, whereas mechanical rotation incurs non-negligible delay in practical deployment. To address this issue, we develop a rotation-delay-aware communication model for finite communication slots, where mechanical rotation directly reduces the time available for data transmission. This model reveals a fundamental tradeoff between the communication gain achieved through RA reorientation and the associated transmission-time loss. Accordingly, we formulate a rotation-delay-aware joint design of RA orientations and transmit beamforming to maximize the minimum effective transmission rate. For the single-user system, under a sufficient condition for strict concavity, the global optimum falls into one of three regimes: no rotation, partial rotation, and full rotation. A closed-form approximation is derived using a second-order expansion. For the multi-user system, we separate rotation-budget selection from fixed-budget max-min signal-to-interference-plus-noise ratio (SINR) optimization. An analytic-gradient zero-forcing (AG-ZF) algorithm is then developed for low-complexity joint RA orientation and rotation-budget optimization. The results show that explicitly accounting for mechanical rotation delay is essential for finite-slot RA-enabled UAV communications, and that the proposed designs effectively balance reconfiguration gain and transmission-time loss while achieving favorable effective-rate performance with low computational complexity.
