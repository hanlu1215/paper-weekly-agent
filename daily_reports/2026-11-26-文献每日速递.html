WorldUAV: Scaling UAV Data Synthesis via World Models

  • 作者Xuewen Liu, Mengjuan Chen, Jing Zhang, Mengzhe Shen, Zekun Xu, Zibo Chen, Zuoxiu Zhang, Yue Dong, Zhikai Li, Qingyi Gu
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-25T02:47:02Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

UAV detection and tracking remain constrained by the scarcity of high-quality training data. Existing game-engine-based synthetic UAV data suffer from a substantial synthetic-to-real gap, while the high cost of manually collected real-world UAV data limits dataset scale. Therefore, we introduce WorldUAV, a high-quality UAV dataset constructed through an efficient framework powered by world models. Specifically, WorldUAV directly integrates UAVs into readily available real-world videos, naturally preserving realistic backgrounds, illumination, and scene dynamics. Leveraging the spatiotemporal priors of world models, it generates UAV videos with plausible motion, pose variations, and cross-frame consistency, effectively narrowing the gap between synthetic and real data. We further develop an agentic pipeline orchestrated by a multimodal large language model that acquires sources, plans UAV trajectories, invokes synthesis, and verifies generated videos, enabling fully automated dataset construction. WorldUAV comprises 6,878 UAV videos and 205,778 UAV images. Experiments show that WorldUAV significantly improves the performance of UAV detectors. Moreover, it substantially reduces dataset construction costs, requiring only 15 seconds and USD 0.0005 API cost per high-quality UAV image. Our work offers both a high-quality dataset and a scalable data generation paradigm for UAV detection and tracking.

A Strongly Subquadratic Algorithm for Unique Assembly Verification in the Abstract Tile Assembly Model

  • 作者Tim Wylie
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-24T17:13:50Z
  • 分类cs.CG
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

We show, for the first time, a strongly sub-quadratic algorithm for Unique Assembly Verification (UAV) in the 2D Abstract Tile Assembly Model (aTAM) with any constant temperature. UAV in the 2D aTAM is solvable in $\widetilde{O}(n^{71/36})$. Further, in 3D, we give a logarithmic improvement for UAV to $O(n^2/\sqrt{\log(n+2)})$, and then show that even for assemblies with a constant height of two in 3D (barely 3D), no strongly sub-quadratic algorithm can exist under the Orthogonal Vectors Conjecture (OVC) by showing it is coOV-hard. Further, we show that UAV for barely 3D assemblies is P-complete and analyze the result with relation to Circuit-SETH. We show that for UAV, any 3D aTAM system can be simulated by a barely 3D aTAM system. This provides a clear conditional separation between the 2D and 3D problems (even with only two layers), and improves the time complexity for one of the most fundamental problems in algorithmic self-assembly in both 2D and 3D.

  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-24T12:06:23Z
  • 分类cs.RO, cs.CG
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Image noise can confound rotation and translation estimates in monocular visual navigation for unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) at low and moderate altitudes, where even small perturbations may cause large errors in translation direction. Inspired by angular cues in long-range visual perception, we propose rotation-only visual odometry (ROVO) for monocular aerial imagery. ROVO estimates pairwise rotations from matched images, robustly averages them over overlapping windows, and aligns consecutive windows through shared frames. By retaining only orientation in the trajectory state, it prevents uncertain landmark depth from propagating to later estimates. In trajectory simulation, sliding-window ROVO achieved a mean orientation error just 10.7% as large as that of the conventional SLAM baseline. On real flight data, ROVO achieved rotation accuracy comparable to ORB-SLAM3 and DPVO under the original Bell412 settings. With a narrower FoV or at higher flight altitude, ROVO reduced relative rotation RMSE by 19.7--69.4% compared with the next-best method in each comparison. Code and simulation tools are available at https://github.com/sssxxxkkkk/ROVO.

**研究思路：**
作者观察到卫星瓦片并非独立样本，它们以固定间距、尺度与朝向铺在地图上，相邻瓦片拼成具有一致度量的连续卫星块；正下视无人机图像与该块之间仅差一个平面刚体变换，即二维平移加平面内旋转。据此，RigidAnchor 编码两幅不同尺寸图像并构建多级相关金字塔，每次迭代以当前估计携带的航拍单元索引金字塔，将所有单元描述子池化为一个隐状态，再由两个解耦头分别预测平移增量与旋转增量，旋转以复合而非相加方式更新。在 Bearing-UAV-90K 上，跨视角位置误差降低 78.6%、航向误差降低 69.7%，HSR@15 提升 19.06 点。

**局限性：**
摘要仅在单一数据集 Bearing-UAV-90K 上给出结果，未说明是否存在真实飞行验证；方法依赖“正下视图像与卫星块仅差平面刚体变换”的假设，大倾角视角、显著高程变化或非平面地形下该假设可能失效；迭代推理的时延与失败收敛情形摘要未说明，跨区域、跨季节泛化性亦未提供足够信息判断。

**值得关注：**
该工作把位置与航向统一为单一几何量求解，为无 GNSS 导航提供可迭代修正的位姿估计范式；相关金字塔加隐状态池化的迭代对齐结构，以及旋转增量“复合而非相加”的解耦头设计，可迁移到其他跨视角配准与视觉伺服任务；评价上同时报告位置、航向误差与 HSR 指标，也提示后续研究应避免只用检索式召回衡量定位性能。
