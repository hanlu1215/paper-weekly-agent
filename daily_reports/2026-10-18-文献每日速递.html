不依赖GPS/GNSS与视觉的风感知无人机自主导航深度强化学习

  • 作者Küçükerdem H.
  • 来源Scopus
  • 发布时间2027-02-01
  • DOI10.1016/j.eswa.2026.134645
  • 分类Expert Systems with Applications, Article, Cited by: 0
  • 论文链接

**研究背景：**
无人机在GPS/GNSS定位反馈缺失或不可用、视觉感知受限的环境中执行自主导航，是拒止环境和具身智能应用中的重要需求。标题所指向的既有路线通常依赖卫星定位或视觉信息，而风扰动会进一步增加状态估计与控制的难度；但摘要未说明现有方法的具体不足及同行方法为何不能充分解决。该文核心想解决的是：如何在无GPS/GNSS位置反馈、无视觉感知条件下，利用风感知与深度强化学习实现无人机自主导航，其意义在于拓展感知退化场景下的导航能力。

**研究思路：**
从问题动机看，作者针对定位反馈和视觉感知缺失叠加风扰的导航困难，提出风感知深度强化学习方法。方法思路大致是将风相关信息纳入强化学习导航策略，使无人机在不依赖上述两类反馈时仍能决策与控制；具体状态、动作、奖励及网络设计摘要未说明。论文试图解决感知缺失与风扰下的自主导航问题，但验证方式和主要结论摘要未说明。

**局限性：**
摘要未提供足够信息判断具体局限性。无法确认其是否仅在仿真中验证、是否依赖特定风场模型或训练假设、能否迁移到真实无人机，以及在不同风速、任务和平台上的泛化性；这些均需全文核实。

**值得关注：**
可关注其“风感知+深度强化学习”的任务设定，以及无GPS/GNSS、无视觉反馈下的导航评价思路；对具身智能中利用非视觉环境线索提升退化感知鲁棒性具有潜在启发，但需结合全文验证仿真到现实的可行性。

机载轻量 LLM 推理的人体感知安全无人机导航

  • 作者Siwei Cai, Yuwei Wu, Vijay Kumar, Lifeng Zhou
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-15T15:43:49Z
  • 分类cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

**研究背景：** 无人机在人群附近作业时，行人、停放的车辆与墙体本应适用不同的安全间距，但传统感知-规划流程把这三类目标一律简化为占据几何，形成所谓“人体感知安全鸿沟”：规划器无法获得按类别区分的安全间距，而且临时遮挡还会把行人相关约束从地图中抹去。论文正是针对这一问题，尝试把视觉观测直接转化为随上下文变化的安全约束。

**研究思路：** 作者用一个共享 BLIP 编码器同时驱动场景描述与文本条件分割，再与深度信息融合完成障碍物三维定位；再由轻量 LLM（Qwen-2.5-0.5B）推断各障碍的风险，并用检索增强生成与语法约束解码把输出限制为规划器可用的 JSON。推理按物体类别每次只执行一次而非逐帧执行：同类结果缓存复用，新类别才触发检索查询；检测结果赋予持久 ID，以便跨遮挡保留在图中。系统在 Jetson Orin NX 上以 10 Hz 运行，回退查询约 0.8 s，且不位于控制关键路径上。Gazebo 低空城市场景中，碰撞事件较几何规划器减少约 3 倍，动态目标下的语义感知降落成功率从 34% 提升到 96%。

**局限性：** 验证仅在 Gazebo 仿真中完成，缺少真实飞行与真实人群实验；224 像素编码器输入限制了远距离小目标的可靠感知，作者以保守行人间距和短期持久化缓解该问题；效果也依赖 Qwen-2.5-0.5B 与检索库在风险判定上的质量，摘要未给出相应的失败案例分析。

**值得关注：** 类别级缓存、语法约束解码与“LLM 置于非关键路径”的设计，提供了一种让大模型以低频方式介入实时机器人决策的可复用范式；语义-几何融合地图与持久目标 ID 对遮挡下的安全规划也有借鉴意义。

Coverage-Preserving Self-Healing in Partially Observable 3D UAV Swarm Networks via an Advection-Diffusion Graph Transformer

  • 作者Dongbin Jiao, Zhihao Li, Zongsheng Geng, Dongdong Zhao, Shengcai Liu, Shi Yan
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-15T15:39:34Z
  • 分类cs.NI
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

This paper investigates the resilience and self-healing of unmanned aerial vehicle swarm communication networks under severe network partitioning in complex and adversarial environments. To address limited global observability after damage, we develop a damage-state inference mechanism that enables the ground control station (GCS) to infer global failures from sparse local reports without transmitting location-sensitive coordinates. To recover multi-cluster partitioned topologies, we then propose AdDiff-GT, a physics-inspired graph Transformer that incorporates advection-diffusion dynamics into a node-supernode hybrid graph. Local message propagation captures intra-cluster structural relationships, while global attention models inter-cluster interactions. A weighted composite loss jointly considers connectivity restoration, maneuvering cost, motion consistency, inter-cluster coordination, and spatial preservation during model training. We further develop a meta-learning strategy for transferable initialization across damage patterns and swarm scales, enabling efficient online GCS adaptation and distributed airborne inference. Simulations on 8,000 partitioned scenarios, including 5,000 large-scale cases, show that AdDiff-GT achieves effective topology recovery and spatial preservation over six representative baselines. Meta-learning reduces online planning overhead with a minor increase in recovery steps, balancing prior knowledge transfer and online adaptation.

CBF-Based Control Allocation for Omnidirectional Tiltrotor Aerial Robots

  • 作者Seobeom Lee, Hyondong Oh
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-15T15:22:25Z
  • 分类cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Actively tilting aerial robots can generate six-dimensional wrenches, but slow servo response can make a desired wrench infeasible during reconfiguration. This paper develops a control barrier function (CBF) allocator that preserves wrench feasibility under bounded servo-configuration mismatch. An exact equivalent-wrench identity connects the thrust required at nominal and actual configurations. For a nonsingular six-channel allocation map, inverse allocation reduces robust wrench containment to twelve thrust margins tightened by an ellipsoidal reserve. Their complete derivatives yield a six-variable servo-rate quadratic program, followed by differential thrust allocation for tracking. A conditional forward-invariance result establishes actual-configuration feasibility under reserve coverage, feasible initialization, and admissible CBF inputs. A complementary MuJoCo study evaluates a measured-angle implementation against geometric, augmented differential, and normalized differential allocation in circular and cartwheel maneuvers, with and without disturbances. The proposed implementation achieves lower position error in all four recorded scenarios, with an attitude tradeoff in circular tracking. During the disturbed cartwheel, the desired wrench is feasible at all 1751 stored samples of continuous rotation; baseline violation rates range from 60.8% to 100%. These comparisons evaluate sampled feasibility; validation of the nominal-trajectory certificate requires nominal-state and reserve-coverage records.

ISAC网络中基于微多普勒的无人机飞行状态估计

  • 作者Henglin Pu, Lu Su, Zhu Han, Athina P. Petropulu, Husheng Li
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-15T04:15:19Z
  • 分类eess.SP
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

**研究背景：**
低空无人机监测需要更丰富的飞行状态信息。现有ISAC研究多聚焦位置与整体速度，难以刻画旋翼动态和机体朝向。论文面向这一空白，利用OFDM-ISAC的微多普勒特征估计旋翼转速与旋翼轴朝向，为低空感知提供超越定位测速的状态信息。

**研究思路：**
作者先在各基站提取微多普勒并估计旋翼转速，再将多站视角相关的微多普勒特征融合，估计共同旋翼轴方向；融合依赖多普勒带宽特征而非复杂信道相位，故无需站间载波相位同步。摘要称三个几何多样视角可局部辨识旋翼轴方向及未知桨尖速度尺度，并用特征感知雷达方程关联叶片RCS、感知距离、发射功率、观测时长与检测/测速精度，分析残余运动和距离徙动对CPI的影响。仿真与实测验证了框架及可行性。

**局限性：**
摘要未说明真实组网规模、杂波与低信噪比条件、多无人机场景及估计精度指标，实验充分性和复杂环境泛化性仍需原文验证。

**值得关注：**
将微多普勒用于旋翼转速与轴向估计，为低空监视和空中机器人状态感知提供超越位置/速度的观测维度；多站带宽特征融合规避相位同步，对分布式ISAC系统设计有借鉴意义。

Resilient and Efficient Aerial Wireless Sensor Networks Using Pinching-Antenna Systems

  • 作者Ahmed A. Al-habob, Octavia A. Dobre, Yindi Jing, Hyundong Shin
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-14T20:55:08Z
  • 分类eess.SP
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

This paper proposes a pinching antenna system to enhance the connectivity and efficiency in aerial wireless networks. The algebraic connectivity of the network's graph is maximized by optimizing the placement of the pinching antenna elements in the uplink and downlink waveguides. The number of deployed unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) is minimized while maintaining a minimum network connectivity indicator. A heuristic pinching antenna placement algorithm is designed to maximize the network connectivity. An iterative UAV deployment algorithm is also designed to find the minimum number of UAVs, their placement locations, and the pinching antenna placement. Simulation results show that the considered pinching antenna system improves network connectivity and efficiency.
