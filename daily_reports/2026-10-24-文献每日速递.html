Cross-Region Cooperation of UAV Swarms Towards Air-Ground ISAC via Hierarchical Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

  • 作者Linghui Miao, Nhan Thanh Nguyen, Shijian Gao
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-22T10:34:17Z
  • 分类eess.SY
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

The limited communication resources and sensing perspectives of a single uncrewed aerial vehicle (UAV) motivate UAV swarms for integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) in air-ground networks. However, in multi-region scenarios, regional UAV allocations may not match vehicle demand, while local observations may lack sufficient diversity for accurate sensing. To address these issues, this paper proposes a cross-region cooperation framework for UAV swarms. For communication, cross-region UAV reallocation reduces service disparities by matching resources to vehicle demand. For sensing, combining UAV observations from different regions increases observation diversity, while adaptive handshaking guided by marginal Cramér--Rao bound (CRB) utilities balances phase synchronization gains against handshaking energy consumption. Joint optimization of UAV positions and transmit power within each region then further improves communication service and sensing performance. These coupled decisions are coordinated by a hierarchical multi-agent reinforcement learning (H-MARL) approach through slow-timescale inter-region UAV reallocation and fast-timescale intra-region UAV control. Under the considered simulation settings, reallocation can improve quality of service (QoS) by over 20 percentage points compared with retaining the initial allocation under severe resource-demand mismatches. Cooperative sensing with adaptive handshaking incurs only about a 5.0% increase in mean CRB relative to perfect synchronization.

动态多无人机边缘网络中的自适应截止时间与能耗感知缓存算法

  • 来源arXiv

**研究背景：** 无人机内容缓存可在地面基础设施不可用或受损时维持内容分发，是应急通信的重要补充。但现有缓存方法多聚焦缓存命中率或平均交付时延，未能联合处理空间异构且时变的需求、内容特定的截止时间，以及无人机间不均衡的通信能耗。论文由此提出动态的截止时间与能耗感知缓存放置与替换问题，同时约束截止时间错过、无人机侧通信能耗与机间能耗不均衡，以期在资源受限的多无人机边缘网络中兼顾时效与能耗公平。

**研究思路：** 作者先设计 DE-UBAC，一种可解释的分层规则方案，融合截止时间优先级、协同放置、能耗感知协调与缓存更新代价；但预定义优先级和阈值难以适应动态耦合的网络条件。为此提出 GRU-DEPPO，用局部与全局 GRU 提取时序表征，配合集中式 PPO actor-critic 实现自适应缓存放置与替换。仿真表明，在对比方案中，GRU-DEPPO 的截止时间错过率与无人机侧总通信能耗最低；相比 DE-UBAC，错过率降低约 61%，机间能耗不均衡降低约 40%。

KinoUAV: A Benchmark for Safe and Stable UAV Vision-Language Navigation with Kinodynamic Modeling

  • 作者Chenyao Yang, Peijin Wang, Yingchao Feng, Wenhui Diao
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-22T02:16:48Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) have recently emerged as important remote sensing platforms, and UAV navigation has shown great potential in remote sensing applications. However, existing methods do not explicitly incorporate kinodynamic modeling or safety distances into trajectory generation, resulting in high absolute yaw rates, frequent collisions, and low navigation success rates, which limit safety and motion stability. To address this challenge, we introduce KinoUAV, a kinodynamic benchmark comprising a dataset and KinoUAV-Agent. First, we develop a hierarchical kinodynamic trajectory generation pipeline that drives UAV through acceleration actions. It uses waypoint chains from global planning and generates trajectories segment by segment under velocity, acceleration, and jerk constraints, reducing oscillations and improving trajectory stability. Furthermore, we introduce directional free-space distance (DFD) labels into the constructed dataset to describe the available clearance for safe motion decisions. The proposed KinoUAV-Agent predicts DFDs and uses them to generate safe actions, decoupling perception from action decision-making. This design enables autonomous obstacle avoidance and improves navigation safety. The benchmark incorporates the proposed Cruise-Seek task, in which the UAV first enters the target region and then searches for the target, reducing the difficulty of target search and improving navigation success. Experiments on KinoUAV show that KinoUAV-Agent outperforms existing methods, achieving the highest success rate while maintaining lower absolute yaw rates and collision rates. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of kinodynamic modeling for safe and stable UAV navigation.

NLOS-Aware TDOA-Based UAV Localization: Cramér-Rao Bounds, Measurement Modeling, and Experimental Validation

  • 作者Cole Dickerson, Saad Masrur, Özgür Özdemir, Ismail Güvenç
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-21T17:13:15Z
  • 分类eess.SP
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Passive time-difference-of-arrival (TDOA) localization is well suited for tracking non-cooperative unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) that do not report their positions. However, its accuracy degrades under the mixed line-of-sight (LOS) and non-line-of-sight (NLOS) propagation that is typical of low-altitude flight. In this paper, we develop NLOS-aware Cramér--Rao lower bounds (CRLBs) and an empirically calibrated measurement model for TDOA-based UAV localization, and we evaluate both with measurements from five UAV flights on the Aerial Experimentation and Research Platform for Advanced Wireless (AERPAW). The analysis yields an identifiability condition that relates the number of sensors to the number of NLOS links, together with a bias-induced error floor that a wider bandwidth cannot remove. The model parameters are estimated by matching simulated and measured error distributions. Experimental results show that the error depends most strongly on the number of sensors in LOS, while a higher altitude and a wider bandwidth lower the median error. Relocating a sensor that is always in NLOS into the open flight area can lower the median horizontal bound by two orders of magnitude if the relocated sensor is in LOS. The fitted model reproduces the median errors of the 2.5 MHz and 5 MHz flights but underestimates the heaviest error tails. Finally, rejecting outlier fixes lowers the error far more than Kalman or particle filtering, because the remaining error is dominated by bias.

Early versus Late RGB-Infrared Fusion for Counter-UAV Detection: A Comparative Study

  • 作者Alberto De Zan, Denis Tavaris, Gian Luca Foresti, Ivan Scagnetto
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-10-21T14:50:48Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

This paper presents a novel strategy for UAV detection through visible (RGB) and infrared (IR) fusion, in order to improve accuracy and precision in harsh environments and weather conditions. In this research, two opposite approaches have been studied and implemented: the first concerns image-level (early) fusion, which merges two different spectra into a single image to which one detector is applied. On the other hand, the second approach, called decision-level (late) fusion, focuses on blending together the output bounding boxes of two different detection pipelines, aiming to create a common result that can be applied to both spectra simultaneously. Both strategies have been extensively tested in a dedicated environment, which included alternating low and high brightness scenes, different backgrounds (sky and ground), distances (5, 25 and 60 m), altitudes and zoom levels (1X, 2X and 4X). These tests allowed for the assessment of actual detection capabilities and system reliability in real-world applications. Furthermore, the two fusion strategies were evaluated and compared over a set of aligned images in order to highlight the better approach of the two. The results indicate that early fusion achieves better raw performance, since it incorporates the thermal information from the beginning of the training process. However, late fusion presents an innovative and useful detection workflow that can be applied in cases of sensor or frame misalignment. Both positive and negative aspects have also been pointed out and discussed, in order to give a more comprehensive overview. Finally, the paper outlines implementation considerations and practical recommendations based on operational priorities. Overall, the findings contribute to the research in RGB-IR fusion modalities, with the goal of enhancing UAV detection capabilities.

**研究思路：** 作者构建适配灾害域的视觉语言模型 Disaster-S1，以选项判别训练，在单次多模态前向中输出答案选项概率分布，实现快速决策；进而提出 DisasterRoute 双系统路由，结合预测置信度与灾害证据，判断接受初始答案还是调用灾害推理模型 DisasterVL 复核。该设计按需分配推理计算，既保留直接决策效率，又通过选择性复核提升整体准确率。在 DisasterBench 上的实验表明，Disaster-S1 以低延迟取得较高准确率，DisasterRoute 以显著低于全推理的延迟进一步提升准确率。

**局限性：** 摘要未提供足够信息判断具体局限性；目前验证集中于 DisasterBench，真实边缘部署、跨灾害场景泛化、路由阈值与证据可靠性等仍需进一步验证。

**值得关注：** 该工作将快慢双系统思想落到 UAV 灾害视觉问答，用置信度与领域证据触发复核，按需分配计算，对边缘具身智能和机器人应急感知决策具有借鉴意义。

无人机搭载STAR-RIS能量收集的混合PPO多目标优化

**研究背景：**
在6G物联网中，无人机搭载STAR-RIS可同时增强通信与射频能量收集，但需联合调控基站波束、STAR-RIS透射/反射系数、功率分割和无人机轨迹；通信、能量收集与移动性强耦合，使问题高度非凸，常规优化难以求解。论文旨在构建多目标框架，在QoS、功率与移动性约束下平衡通信性能与能量收集。

**研究思路：**
作者建立加权多目标优化问题，并提出混合近端策略优化（H-PPO）。其actor-critic用分类策略处理离散无人机移动，用Beta分布策略处理连续通信控制变量，并以两阶段奖励保障QoS可行性、兼顾和速率、能效与收集能量。仿真与PPO、TD3对比显示，H-PPO收敛分别约快66%和60%，训练更稳定，且在不同QoS、STAR-RIS尺寸、发射功率和用户密度下平衡通信与能量收集。

**局限性：**
摘要仅报告仿真结果，未说明真实系统验证、信道与能耗模型假设、算法复杂度及跨场景泛化能力；具体局限仍需全文实验支撑。

**值得关注：**
其混合离散—连续动作的PPO设计、Beta策略和两阶段奖励，为无人机/机器人轨迹与通信资源联合控制、多约束多目标权衡提供了可借鉴范式，评价上突出收敛稳定性与QoS可行性。
