Zonotope-Based Active Exposure of Stealthy Deception Attacks in Sensor-Fusion Systems

  • 作者Meiqi Tian, Shuo Li, Bingzhuo Zhong
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-09-02T13:31:45Z
  • 分类eess.SY
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

This paper investigates the stealthy attack detection for sensor-fusion cyber-physical systems with unknown-but-bounded noises through the control channel. The detection framework is particularly applicable to sensor-fusion scenarios in which multiple suspicious sensors contributing to the fused estimate may be compromised simultaneously. First, we construct an admissible output set using secure sensors and an attack output set for each attack hypothesis. Then, we introduce a receding-horizon optimization framework to design exposure inputs, namely bounded auxiliary control perturbations injected through the control channel, so as to enlarge the separation between the admissible output set and the attack output sets according to the separation tendency. A sufficient detection condition is further derived, showing that set separation guarantees detectability of the compromised sensors. Moreover, an offline exposure budget guidance is developed to support budget selection before online exposure starts. Simulations on a UAV navigation system under stealthy GNSS and LiDAR attacks validate the proposed method.

应急响应中连接式无人机通信桥的组合优化

  • 作者Matteo Vandelli, Daniele Dragoni
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-09-02T13:14:12Z
  • 分类quant-ph, math.OC
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

**研究背景：**
自然灾害常导致地面通信基础设施损毁，严重阻碍救援行动。为快速恢复灾区通信，利用搭载5G天线的无人机组建飞行自组网成为一种有前景的应急方案，但如何将其战略性地部署到候选站点以提供有效覆盖，是一个关键的组合优化难题。该问题需要同时权衡信号覆盖最大化、干扰抑制与网络连通性，现有经典求解方法在站点数量增加时面临严峻的计算扩展性瓶颈，难以满足应急救援对实时性的严苛要求，这促使研究者探索包括量子计算在内的新型求解范式。

**研究思路：**
作者将无人机部署问题形式化为整数二次规划（IQP），目标是在保证网络连通的前提下最大化信号覆盖并最小化多频段干扰。研究采用CPLEX求解器获取数值解，并通过扩展性分析揭示了随站点数量增长而呈指数上升的求解时间（TTS），指出了其在紧急场景中的局限性。为兼顾可行性与实时性，作者探讨了设置求解时限以获取次优解、同时保持连通性的实用策略。鉴于经典方法的不足，问题被重构为适用于量子算法的QUBO形式，并通过高性能计算仿真验证了量子绝热算法（QAA）在小规模实例上的求解准确性与应用潜力。

**局限性：**
摘要中未提供足够信息判断具体局限性。目前仅涉及小规模实例的量子算法模拟验证，缺乏在真实量子硬件上的测试；UAV动态重部署与网络配置的复杂性，以及对特定仿真环境和模型假设的依赖程度，仍需进一步探究。

**值得关注：**
该研究为应急场景下机器人系统的资源调度提供了严谨的数学建模与算法设计范本，其QP→QUBO的转化思路可迁移至其他组合优化问题。其对经典求解器可扩展性瓶颈的量化分析，以及对量子计算潜力的探索，为未来AI驱动的实时决策系统，特别是将量子优化算法与智能无人机集群相结合用于灾害响应的研究趋势提供了重要启示。

**研究背景：**
热红外航拍车辆检测中，真实红外数据稀缺严重制约视觉AI发展，而无人机RGB影像数据充足。将丰富的RGB数据翻译为红外图像以扩充训练集成为自然思路，但红外特征中包含引擎热量等不可观测信息，导致跨模态映射学习困难。现有生成方法能否克服这一模态差异，为未知目标域生成有效红外训练数据，是本研究关注的核心问题。

**研究思路：**
研究动机源于红外数据匮乏阻碍航拍车辆检测。方法上，作者在配对RGB-IR源数据集上训练翻译器，再对保留目标域的RGB训练图像进行翻译生成合成红外数据。测试了监督GAN、ControlNet扩散模型和LoRA微调的基础模型编辑等方法。合成红外数据用于训练RF-DETR检测器，并在五个航拍数据集的未见IR测试集上评估。结果表明合成红外数据优于RGB和灰度基线；Stable Diffusion 3.5结合ControlNet效果最佳，在Kust4K和VTUAV上将mAP分别从50.8提升至60.1、从25.6提升至38.4；增加输出多样性也能带来额外收益。

**局限性：**
摘要显示合成红外数据与真实目标域红外数据之间仍存在性能差距。实验主要在Kust4K和VTUAV作为目标域的五数据集框架下完成，其他未见域上的泛化效果尚未充分验证。摘要未提供足够信息判断具体局限性。

**值得关注：**
该研究验证了现代扩散模型在跨模态数据生成中的实用价值，为红外数据稀缺场景提供了可行的数据增强路线。其“源域训练翻译器、目标域生成合成数据”的流程设计可供其他模态稀缺的感知任务借鉴，对无人机侦察、自动驾驶夜视感知等具身智能应用具有参考意义。

从多鱼眼感知到全景感知：面向超低空无人机的视差感知机载平台

  • 作者Dun Dai, Ze Lu, Cheng He, Yaowen Wang, Quan Quan
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-09-02T09:04:52Z
  • 分类cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

**研究背景：**
超低空无人机在建筑、植被等复杂环境中飞行时，需要获得环绕式视觉感知以保障安全。传统多相机拼接往往忽略重叠区域的视差问题，导致远处景物错位，难以支撑下游感知任务。论文旨在构建一个将多路鱼眼视觉流实时合成为统一全景接口的机载平台，为超低空无人机的全景感知提供可复用、可落地的系统方案。

**研究思路：**
作者针对多鱼眼实时全景拼接中的视差难题，提出一种视差感知的机载拼接平台。方法上，平台集成四路同步鱼眼相机与嵌入式计算单元，通过按重叠区域选择投影深度、控制拼接接缝并融合光度信息，将四路图像输出为1280×640的等距柱状全景图。拼接管线提供两种配置：高精度模式采用内容自适应接缝搜索与验证门控残差网格，部署模式则通过裕度门控的接缝更新维持传感器速率运行。实验基于18个野外序列、超5万组四视图像进行，高精度模式相比固定深度方法将远场特征错位P90误差降低41.6%；在20Hz回放测试中，部署模式达到19.99帧/秒、平均功耗13.29W；八扇区全景采样在白天场景下视觉地点识别Recall@5达90.8%。结果表明该平台实现了视差感知拼接、嵌入式实时执行与下游视觉接口的统一。

**局限性：**
摘要未提供足够信息判断具体局限性。实验集中于日间场景与特定机载硬件，其夜间、恶劣天气或不同机型下的鲁棒性尚待说明；Recall@5等指标能否推广至其他下游任务也缺少证据。

**值得关注：**
该工作将视差建模、嵌入式实时性与开放全景接口结合，为超低空无人机的感知系统设计提供了可借鉴的整机集成范式。其开源的拼接软件与评估方法，可服务于机器人全景感知、具身导航及自动驾驶等领域的后续研究与复现。

Enhancing Vehicular Network Performance Through Integrated RSU and UAV Deployment

  • 作者Muhammad Ismail, Syed Luqman Shah, Fazal Muhammad, Zeeshan Shafiq
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-09-02T08:50:50Z
  • 分类eess.SP
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

The increasing density of connected vehicles can place substantial pressure on fixed roadside infrastructure, particularly when the available communication resources become insufficient to accommodate temporary traffic surges. This paper investigates auxiliary unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) assistance as a flexible mechanism for improving service availability and throughput in vehicular networks. Two representative network configurations are considered. In the first, an auxiliary UAV (UAVa) supplements two fixed roadside units (RSUs), whereas in the second, UAVa assists a heterogeneous infrastructure comprising one RSU and one UAV. Vehicle service is determined according to node coverage, the line-of-sight (LoS) probability of aerial links, and a prescribed signal-to-interference-plus-noise ratio (SINR) requirement. The resulting framework enables UAVa to accommodate eligible vehicles that cannot be adequately served by the primary infrastructure as the network load increases. Simulation results show that, under the considered configurations, the aerial nodes benefit from more favorable propagation conditions and achieve higher throughput than the fixed terrestrial RSU. Moreover, the introduction of UAVa increases the available service capacity under high vehicular loads, both for a purely terrestrial baseline and for a network already supported by an aerial node. These results demonstrate the potential of auxiliary UAV assistance as a flexible load-relief mechanism for capacity-constrained vehicular networks.

ISAC with Co-Prime Arrays: Virtual-Aperture Sensing and uplink downlink communications

  • 作者Jing Zhang, Yuxiao Liu, Jiayi Sun, Junliang Ye, Derrick Wing Kwan Ng
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-09-02T01:30:05Z
  • 分类cs.IT
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) enables simultaneous communication and environmental sensing in unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) networks, but its performance is constrained by the physical antenna aperture and residual self-interference (SI) in full-duplex (FD) sensing. To address these issues, we propose a shared-aperture ISAC architecture in which a sparse co-prime array (CPA) is embedded in a uniform linear array (ULA) grid for FD sensing, while the remaining antenna positions support time-division duplexing (TDD) communication. We characterize the sensing performance through an order-wise Cramer-Rao bound (CRB) analysis, showing that the CPA achieves a stronger asymptotic sensing gain than the partitioned ULA benchmark in both single-target and nondegenerate multi-target scenarios. We further reveal a space-time sampling tradeoff under the same physical aperture. Based on the proposed architecture, we formulate a non-convex joint resource allocation problem that maximizes the weighted downlink-uplink sum rate by jointly designing the sensing transmit covariance, downlink precoder, and uplink receive beamformers under sensing accuracy, BS transmit-power, communication QoS, and residual SI constraints. An alternating-optimization-based algorithm is developed. Simulations demonstrate consistent performance gains over the considered baselines and confirm the complementary benefits of the CPA virtual aperture and sensing covariance optimization.

Quantum-Based k-Coverage Optimization for UAV-Aided Search and Rescue Missions

  • 作者Halim Lee, Suhui Jeong, Na Young Kim, Jiwon Seo
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-09-01T22:58:44Z
  • 分类eess.SY
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

In large-scale disaster scenarios, rapid localization of missing persons is a critical challenge for search-and-rescue (SAR) operations. Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) equipped with radio frequency (RF) receivers can support RF-based localization by collecting signals emitted from mobile devices at spatially distributed sensing locations. This paper addresses the resulting waypoint-selection problem: determining a minimum set of UAV waypoints that provides at least threefold coverage of every potential target location. We formulate this task as an extended k-coverage problem that independently defines the UAV-navigable and target regions, and derive an exact-penalty quadratic unconstrained binary optimization (QUBO) formulation with a sufficient penalty condition that preserves feasibility and minimum waypoint cardinality. The QUBO is mapped to an Ising-form cost Hamiltonian and evaluated using the quantum approximate optimization algorithm (QAOA) on both a noise-free simulator and IBM's 127-qubit Eagle processor. On the tested simulator instances, QAOA recovers the known minimum-cardinality solutions. Across the rectangular hardware test cases, the mean 3-coverage ratio exceeded 95%. In the campus-scale evaluation, ten hardware executions achieved 99.3% mean 3-coverage with a 90% feasible-run rate, while the shortest feasible flight path was up to 37.0% shorter than those of the deterministic grid-based baselines. Additional comparisons with classical optimization and learning-based baselines are provided, together with computational and quantum-resource analyses for larger generated instances. These results establish an exact QUBO representation for RF-based SAR waypoint selection and characterize its implementation on current gate-based quantum hardware.

Curriculum-Guided Reinforcement Learning for Energy-Efficient UAV-ISAC in Post-Disaster Search-and-Rescue Operations

  • 作者Tai-You Guo, Chuan-Chi Lai
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-09-01T18:31:14Z
  • 分类cs.NI, eess.SY
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Uncrewed aerial vehicles (UAVs) are promising platforms for integrated sensing and communication (ISAC), but their limited onboard energy creates a strong coupling among sensing accuracy, communication quality, and propulsion cost. This paper proposes a curriculum-guided soft actor-critic (CG-SAC) framework with propulsion-aware reward shaping for energy-efficient UAV-ISAC, jointly optimizing the 3D trajectory, communication-sensing power split, and per-user power allocation. A rotary-wing propulsion model is incorporated to derive a closed-form propulsion-economic cruising speed, which is used to construct a propulsion-aware speed-shaping term within a normalized composite reward together with navigation, node-visiting, energy-efficiency, and constraint-penalty terms. A log-linear curriculum progressively tightens the communication, sensing, and proximity requirements during training. Across 2000 randomized scenarios, CG-SAC achieves an average energy efficiency of 0.72 Mbits/J, substantially outperforming the evaluated DRL baselines. Among successfully completed missions, it requires 107.6 steps on average, corresponding to a 66%--82% reduction in flight steps relative to the baselines. Crucially, the learned policy exhibits mission-aware speed adaptation by decelerating near service points and accelerating during transit, while achieving a 99.6% communication-rate satisfaction ratio at service instants. Ablation results further demonstrate the complementary roles of the reward components in balancing mission feasibility and energy efficiency.

Evidential Deep Learning for Multi-Modal Anti-UAV Detection

  • 作者Dmitry Golovchits, Seyed Sahand Mohammadi Ziabari, Ali Mohammed Mansoor Alsahag
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-09-01T18:09:46Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Anti-UAV systems increasingly fuse multiple sensors, yet their detection heads provide no per-modality reliability signal. This study evaluates whether evidential deep learning (EDL) heads, Dempster-Shafer (DS) evidence fusion, and uncertainty-driven temporal sensor gating improve anti-UAV detection through a controlled ablation on three benchmarks: thermal tracking (AntiUAV600), RGB-audio-RF classification (TRIDENT), and RGB-IR tracking (MM-UAV). The EDL training objective improves accuracy over retrained sigmoid baselines (+5.9 percentage points in accuracy and a tripled tracker-on-absent rate in E1; +4.8 percentage points in classification accuracy in E2, surviving a clip-clustered bootstrap, p = 0.011) and ranks classification errors substantially better (entropy UAUC approximately 0.94 vs. 0.51). The remaining components do not support their respective hypotheses. DS fusion does not outperform simple probability averaging. Dirichlet vacuity adds no ranking power beyond predictive entropy and inverts at the detection level, where extreme background imbalance causes it to encode class membership rather than error likelihood, a failure also observed for entropy and sigmoid confidence. Temporal gating preserves accuracy only when nearly inactive and yields no realised latency saving on shared-backbone hardware. The benefit of evidential learning therefore arises primarily from its training objective rather than its uncertainty estimate; a crop-level control further localises the detection-level breakdown to anchor-level evaluation rather than the learned representation.

无人机热成像用于惰性弹药筛查：多阶段数据集构建、目标检测与实践建议

  • 作者Chad Melton, PhD., Annabelle Kelton
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-09-01T18:07:41Z
  • 分类cs.CV, cs.DB
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

**研究背景：**
未爆弹药持续威胁全球多个地区的民用通行、农业生产与基础设施重建。传统人工排查效率低、风险高，而无人机热成像可提供非接触式大范围筛查能力。然而，现有研究多聚焦单一场景或缺乏系统性的实地数据。该领域面临的主要不足包括：热成像数据受地表材质、植被遮挡和光照条件影响大，标注数据稀缺，且缺乏覆盖季节、高度、地表类型等变量的公开多阶段数据集。因此，构建贴近真实作业场景的数据集并验证自动化目标检测模型的可行性，对于人道主义排雷和后续技术调查具有重要意义。

**研究思路：**
作者针对惰性未爆弹药的无人机热成像筛查问题，构建了一套包含四个野外实验阶段的图像数据集。数据采集覆盖夏冬两季，涉及短草、高植被、砾石、覆盖物、岩石、堆肥和压实地面等多种地表条件，并在33米和15米两种飞行高度下拍摄。最终形成5,855对热图像与标签，包含918张正样本和4,937张背景图像。作者使用YOLOv11l与RT-DETR-R50两种目标检测算法进行训练和评估，以验证自动化候选目标检测的可行性。主要结论包括：建议同时采集热成像与RGB图像，纳入多样地表和纯背景图像，关注太阳照射变化时段，平衡覆盖范围与目标像素数量，使用本地代表性数据校准模型，并保留合格人工审核环节。该方法的预期用途是辅助筛查和优先级排序，而非独立完成清障。

**局限性：**
摘要中未提供足够信息判断具体局限性。但可推断，数据采集集中于美国田纳西州的特定环境，场景多样性与全球真实受污染区域之间仍可能存在差距。此外，研究仅使用惰性弹药，真实未爆弹药的复杂状态可能带来额外不确定性。模型在野外新场景中的泛化能力以及人工审核环节的效率指标也未被摘要展开说明。

**值得关注：**
这项研究展示了无人机热成像在危险环境感知任务中的系统化应用范式。其多阶段数据采集策略（覆盖季节、地表、高度）为构建具身智能感知数据集提供了参考。将训练数据中的正样本与纯背景图像按比例处理、并对RGB与热成像进行联合采集的思路，对多模态感知与模型校准具有借鉴意义。该方法强调“筛查辅助”而非全自动清障，体现了在实际高风险作业中人机协同、保留人工决策的务实设计理念，这对明确自主系统在安全关键任务中的边界具有启发性。

TriSAR: Task Coordination and Collision Avoidance for Aerial Robot Teams in Disaster Response

  • 作者Aditya Anil Kapile, Pedro Machado, Isibor Kennedy Ihianle
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-09-01T18:02:12Z
  • 分类cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Multi-Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) disaster-response systems require coordinated task assignment and local trajectory control, yet the individual and combined contributions of these coordination layers to mission efficiency and operational safety remain insufficiently characterised under controlled experimental conditions. TriSAR is evaluated as a five-UAV coordination system operating in a physics-based Gazebo simulation of an earthquake-damaged urban environment. A 2 x 2 factorial design compares two task-allocation strategies (Genetic Algorithm and greedy fitness-based allocation) with reactive collision avoidance enabled or disabled. Each of the four configurations was evaluated over 30 stochastic episodes in a common scenario of five UAVs and eight targets. Under greedy allocation, enabling repulsion eliminated recorded collision-threshold violations, confirmed by a Mann-Whitney test (U = 885, p = 4.03 x 10^-12, rank-biserial r = 0.97). Under GA allocation, the same protective effect was confirmed (U = 675, p = 1.26 x 10^-5, rank-biserial r = 0.50). For mission-efficiency metrics, GA-based allocation showed no statistically detectable advantage over greedy allocation when repulsion was enabled, but a significant advantage in steps, path length, and energy when repulsion was disabled (Welch's t-tests, |g| between 0.92 and 1.76). These results show that reactive repulsion provides a substantial, allocation-dependent safety benefit, while the additional computational complexity of GA-based task allocation yields a detectable mission-efficiency benefit only when repulsion is disabled.

Vision-Based Leader-Follower Formation Control for Cooperative UAVs in GPS-Degraded Environments

  • 作者Deekshitha Angadi, Naveena Budda, Vikas Agarwal, Rojesh Arunkumar Mulasa, Ravi Killamsetty, Mohamed Samshad, Narsimlu Kemsaram
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-09-01T15:35:38Z
  • 分类cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Cooperation in multi-UAV systems requires reliable relative perception so that follower vehicles can maintain formation and continue their mission safely even when absolute positioning sensors degrade or fail. This paper presents a vision-based cooperative formation framework running on a follower UAV that uses a front-facing RGB-D camera to detect, track, and localize a leader UAV in real-time. A lightweight YOLO-based detector is trained on a dedicated drone dataset and deployed onboard to predict leader bounding boxes, which are then fused with depth information via a pinhole camera model to estimate the leader's relative pose. These estimates provide a leader-follower position controller and can also be used as a backup when GPS or external localization is unavailable. This framework is implemented as a set of ROS nodes and evaluated in a physics-based multi-UAV simulation built on XTDrone, with sensor noise and communication dropouts. We evaluate detection accuracy, runtime, and formation-keeping error under nominal conditions and under simulated failures of the positioning sensors. The results show that the proposed framework maintains stable leader-follower formations with reasonable computational cost and provides a practical basis for extending vision-based cooperative formation control to real-world multi-UAV systems.

**研究背景：**
事件相机以低延迟和高时间分辨率记录视觉变化，在无人机（UAV）高速运动场景中具有独特优势，但如何利用事件流实现可靠的目标框预测仍是挑战。现有方法多直接学习事件到目标状态的映射，或单纯依赖运动模型，前者易受数据偏差影响，后者难以应对加速度等非线性变化。论文针对FRED事件相机数据集上的短中期无人机框预测问题，指出传统物理模型虽然稳健但存在系统偏差，而纯数据驱动方法又可能利用数据集特有的运动先验而非真实视觉证据。作者希望构建一种既能保留物理模型可解释性、又能通过视觉事件修正预测偏差的混合框架。

**研究思路：**
论文以恒定速度卡尔曼滤波器作为强物理基线，在全中心-尺寸框状态上运行，并训练一个残差模型，从近期框历史、滤波状态特征和局部事件表征中预测类似加速度的修正量。该残差公式在基线上持续提升性能，其中事件条件模型在所有评测方法中表现最强。摘要进一步指出，部分残差目标仅由锚点位置和速度即可预测，说明FRED基准结果可能混合了视觉证据与数据集特有的运动先验。为此作者引入去相关子集作为诊断压力测试，结果表明即使削弱位置和速度捷径，事件条件残差模型仍保留有效预测信号，验证了事件信息对预测的真实贡献。

**局限性：**
摘要未提供足够信息判断具体局限性。论文主要在FRED事件相机数据集上验证，其结论是否适用于其他场景、其他类型事件数据或真实飞行控制任务尚不明确；去相关子集构建方式及残差模型在更复杂机动下的表现也未在摘要中详述。

**值得关注：**
该研究提供了一个“物理模型+残差学习融合事件流”的清晰范式，可用于无人机运动预测、机器人目标跟踪等实时任务。其将卡尔曼滤波作为可解释基线、用事件流修正加速度残差的思路，对具身智能中融合传统估计器与神经网络的混合设计具有启发意义。同时，通过去相关子集诊断数据捷径的方法值得借鉴，有助于区分模型究竟是学到了视觉本质还是数据集偏差，为构建更可靠的评测基准提供了新视角。

Standardized benchmarks have played a central role in advancing robot manipulation learning, yet most focus on ground-supported manipulation systems, which limits their applicability to dynamics-critical domains such as aerial manipulation (AM). AM presents distinct system-level challenges, including environmental disturbances, coupled dynamics between the manipulator and floating base, and constrained degrees of freedom. Consequently, task performance depends jointly on robot embodiment, low-level control, and high-level policy design. We introduce AM-Bench, a modular simulation suite and benchmark for multirotor-based AM policy learning. AM-Bench includes representative embodiments spanning underactuated, fully actuated, and overactuated systems, 12 tasks across contact, transport, and constrained interaction, configurable aerodynamic disturbances and actuator saturation, standard low-level controllers, and baseline policy-learning algorithms. This paper presents FAIRY, a full-stack smart-agriculture agent system developed for and deployed to an operating soybean research farm at Harbin Institute of Technology's smart-agriculture site.
