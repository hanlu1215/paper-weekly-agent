Site-specific Channel Modeling Based on Remote-Sensing Maps for 6G Space--Air--Ground Digital Twins

  • 作者Peijie Liu, Pan Tang, Jianhua Zhang, Lei Tian, Bin Ao, Boyang He, Hao Zheng
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-08-31T02:37:59Z
  • 分类eess.SP
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Site-specific channel models are essential for wireless digital twins of 6G space--air--ground communication systems. However, 3D maps are difficult to obtain over wide areas, which limits large-area site-specific channel modeling. To address this issue, this paper proposes a remote-sensing-based augmented ray-tracing channel modeling framework. The framework comprises a deterministic RT branch, a measurement-statistical branch, and an RT augmentation branch. To overcome the difficulty of acquiring large-area 3D maps, the deterministic RT branch reconstructs a 3D RT scene from satellite remote-sensing imagery and calibrates its electromagnetic material parameters using measured path loss. To provide the statistical parameters required for RT augmentation, the measurement-statistical branch establishes the marginal distributions and interparameter dependence models of the channel parameters. Specifically, a wideband UAV channel measurement campaign is conducted at 4.60 GHz, and a proposed multipath estimation method estimates the complex amplitudes, delays, and Doppler shifts of the measured multipath. To bridge the gap between RT predictions and measurements, the RT augmentation branch organizes the RT multipath into LoS, LoS-tail, and NLoS components, generates additional short-delay LoS-tail paths, and reallocates the component and path powers according to the measurement-derived statistics while preserving the total RT received power. The validation results show that the proposed framework reduces the path loss RMSE from 5.45 to 4.35 dB and, relative to calibrated RT, decreases the RMS delay spread and normalized Doppler spread RMSEs by 53.03 and 26.48, respectively. The proposed framework provides a site-specific channel modeling approach for 6G space--air--ground digital-twin studies.

CedarCypress3D：柳杉扁柏人工林单木无人机激光雷达标注数据集

  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-08-31T02:06:39Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

**研究背景：**
无人机搭载激光雷达（UAV-LiDAR）获取的单木测量数据，对森林清查、生态系统监测和可持续经营具有重要价值。随着机器学习方法的发展，点云分割等任务对高质量标注数据集的需求日益迫切。然而，当前公开可用的温带森林UAV-LiDAR标注数据集十分有限，制约了相关算法的开发与评测。针对这一数据缺口，该论文构建并发布了日本柳杉与扁柏人工林的单木级标注数据集CedarCypress3D，填补了温带人工林场景下公开数据的不足。

**研究思路：**
研究动机源于个体树分割等点云方法对标注数据的依赖与现有公开数据稀缺之间的矛盾。数据集涵盖两处地形特征不同的样地共34个圆形样区，包含UAV-LiDAR点云和实地调查数据，并提供了其中22个样区的地面激光雷达点云。共对1,627棵树进行了实地测量，并在UAV-LiDAR点云中完成对应单木的人工标注；对拥有地面激光雷达数据的样区，还额外为树点赋予了树干/非树干的语义标签。该数据集用于支持单木实例分割和语义分割方法的开发与评估，也可服务于树木属性预测和多平台激光雷达融合分析。论文通过公开数据集的发布形式展示其验证价值。

**局限性：**
摘要中未提供足够信息判断具体局限性。不过，数据仅涵盖日本柳杉和扁柏两种针叶树种，且样区分布局限于两个日本人工林地点，其树种多样性、地形条件和经营管理方式的覆盖面仍有待扩展，跨区域和跨树种的泛化能力尚需后续验证。

**值得关注：**
该数据集为林业点云感知研究提供了难得的基准资源，尤其在单木实例分割任务上，可支撑深度学习方法在自然稀疏点云场景下的训练与评测。其“UAV-LiDAR+地面实测+部分地面激光雷达多平台融合”的设计思路，对多传感器联合分析、跨平台点云配准等研究方向具有参考价值，也可为森林机器人巡检、自主测量等应用提供数据基础。

VeloBins: Learning Velocity and Its Uncertainty via Bins and Error-Conditioned Gaussian Labels for Aerial Inertial Odometry

  • 作者Maulana Bisyir Azhari, Seungwook Lee, Donghun Han, Sung Jun Park, David Hyunchul Shim
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-08-30T11:00:45Z
  • 分类cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Inertial odometry (IO) is critical for aerial robots, where aggressive maneuvers and poor lighting degrade visual sensors. Recent learning-based IO methods improve traditional integration-based approaches by learning motion priors from IMU and platform-specific sensors, then fusing the predictions within an extended Kalman filter. However, learning velocity through regression is difficult, while jointly estimating uncertainty with a separate decoder and negative log-likelihood (NLL) loss further complicates training and can lead to over-confident estimates. We introduce VeloBins, which reformulates velocity regression as classification over discretized velocity bins. We decode both the velocity from the bin distribution's expectation and the uncertainty from its variance, removing the need for a separate uncertainty decoder. We further supervise the uncertainty explicitly using an error-conditioned Gaussian label centered at the ground-truth velocity, with a standard deviation set to the velocity error. We evaluate VeloBins on four aerial datasets, ranging from free-form aggressive flights and a 27 g nano-quadrotor to drone racing at over 21~m/s. VeloBins achieves the lowest average errors on all four datasets, reducing velocity, relative trajectory, and absolute trajectory errors by 3-27%, 8-40%, and 6-53%, respectively, compared with the strongest baseline. Notably, the proposed supervision achieves the lowest NLL and best filter consistency despite never optimizing an NLL loss. The code will be available upon acceptance.

不确定性感知的多模态反无人机检测：基于证据融合与冲突折扣信念聚合

  • 作者Sharanda Suttorp, Seyed Sahand Mohammadi Ziabari, Ali Mohammed Mansour Alsahag
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-08-29T12:48:22Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

**研究背景：**
反无人机感知系统需要在遮挡、快速运动或传感器失效等退化条件下保持可靠。现有多模态反无人机系统通常以确定性方式融合RGB与热成像流，既未建模预测不确定性，也无法在模态间意见不一致时表达怀疑。尽管证据深度学习（EDL）可在单次前向传播中输出校准的不确定性，且EDTC方法已将其用于单热成像感知，但跨模态证据融合仍未解决。本文针对该缺口，探索将不确定性建模引入多模态反无人机检测。

**研究思路：**
作者将EDTC扩展至RGB-热成像多模态感知，提出折扣信念融合（DBF），先将模态间冲突转化为不确定性质量，再聚合各流意见，并以低不确定性模态解析检测框。在Anti-UAV基准上，多模态融合优于任一单模态流（测试准确率0.670，优于红外0.604和RGB 0.598），且达到实时速度（至少38 FPS）。然而，DBF与未折扣平均在结果上几乎无差异，因为该基准中模态间冲突接近零，使折扣步骤失效；融合不确定性校准良好（ECE 0.057），但作为定位失败检测器的能力弱于空间方差（AUROC 0.626对0.739）。作者将这一“零结果”归因于基准结构本身。

**局限性：**
摘要明确指出论文主要限制来自基准数据特性：样本中目标普遍存在且缺失编码方式粗糙，共同压制了模态间冲突，导致冲突感知融合的优势无法体现。因此DBF的有效性在存在显著模态冲突的场景中尚未得到验证。此外，摘要未提供跨数据集泛化、真实系统部署或与其他不确定性方法对比的信息。

**值得关注：**
该工作为多模态感知中的不确定性建模提供了新思路，尤其是将模态间分歧显式转化为不确定性质量的做法，对机器人、自动驾驶等依赖多传感器融合的鲁棒感知系统具有启发意义。其“零结果”诊断也提醒研究者注意数据集结构对融合方法评价的影响，建议未来在冲突更充分的场景中重新审视冲突感知融合的收益。

Designing, Deployment and Field Testing of C2Stack for Networked Intelligent Software-Defined UAVs

  • 作者Maxwell McManus, Zhaoxi Zhang, Sidharth Santhi Nivas, Yuqing Cui, Prem Sagar Pattanshetty Vasanth Kumar, Chenzhi Zhao, Nicholas Mastronarde, George Sklivanitis, Dimitris Pados, Elizabeth Serena Bentley, Zhangyu Guan
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-08-28T20:50:10Z
  • 分类eess.SY
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) are emerging as critical enablers of next-generation wireless networking and autonomous systems. Despite their potential, deploying and testing networked UAV systems in real-world environments remains challenging, largely due to the absence of well-developed, end-to-end, ready-to-use protocol stacks. To fill this gap, we present C2Stack, a configurable protocol stack and experimental framework designed for real-time control, evaluation, and optimization of UAV networks. C2Stack incorporates a modular control plane, referred to as the~C2Stack Network Operating System (CNOS), alongside a programmable data plane that exposes APIs for cross-layer algorithm development, digital twin integration, and autonomous swarm control. In this article, we share our experience with the deployment and testing of C2Stack. We implemented C2Stack on a custom UAV swarm platform that integrates multiprocessor system-on-chip (MPSoC) radios with Intel NUC computing modules, enabling interoperability with various RF front ends. Field trials were conducted in both netted environments and large-scale outdoor test ranges, focusing on two representative use cases: (i) network utility maximization through online reinforcement learning, and (ii) collaborative interference source localization. The experiments demonstrate the feasibility of real-time, data-driven optimization in dynamic aerial environments, while also revealing practical challenges in field deployments of networked UAV systems, including power constraints, sensing limitations, and deployment logistics. We have made C2Stack source code available to the community under the MIT License, with the goal of establishing it as a foundational framework for experimental research on intelligent networked aerial systems.

GeoFF3D：面向大规模无人机测绘的坐标锚定前馈重建

  • 作者Xiang Yang, Yongli Wang, Yunsheng Zhang, Jun Li, Hao Chen, Haifeng Li
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-08-28T12:51:57Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

**研究背景：**
现有前馈三维重建方法通常只能处理数量受限的图像，并在局部或内部归一化坐标系中恢复相机位姿与几何结构，难以直接扩展到大规模无人机测绘任务。虽然可通过分块处理和Sim(3)对齐来实现多块聚合，但当无人机轨迹近似共线时，全Sim(3)对齐会变得不稳定。GeoFF3D针对这一问题，提出将地理参考坐标直接锚定到重建模型中，从而在重力对齐的Z-up度量坐标系中输出结果，避免了对齐不稳定的困扰，为大范围无人机地图构建提供更可靠的解决方案。

**研究思路：**
作者提出GeoFF3D，其核心是将坐标锚定模型与空间大规模重建框架（SLRF）相结合。模型利用地理参考的相机平移和可选的几何先验，直接预测相机位姿和稠密点图；SLRF则将图像划分为空间重叠的分块，传播共享视角先验，并分层聚合局部重建结果，且可适用于不同的有界视角模型。在九个航空测绘区块上，GeoFF3D取得了最佳平均重建质量，F@5从Pi3X+SLRF的0.829提升至0.877；在长序列UAVScenes上达到0.848，明显优于对比方法。摘要显示，GeoFF3D可在约五分钟内重建2000张图像，验证了其可扩展性和鲁棒性。

**局限性：**
摘要中未提供足够信息判断具体局限性，例如对地理参考相机平移的依赖程度、SLRF在不同数据分布下的泛化能力，以及与其他方法在更多场景下的对比均未详细说明。

**值得关注：**
该工作展示了将地理坐标先验融入前馈重建模型的思路，有助于缓解大尺度场景中位姿对齐不稳定的问题；其分块传播与分层聚合框架也具备模块化特点，可迁移到其他重建模型。对于无人机测绘、自动驾驶高精地图构建以及机器人在大范围环境中的空间感知任务，GeoFF3D提供了一种兼顾精度与效率的系统设计范式，值得后续研究借鉴。

SonicNudge: Controlled Displacement of Hovering UAVs via Estimator-Controller Coupling

  • 作者Shaocheng Luo, Ashir Raza, Haocheng Meng, David Hunt, Miroslav Pajic
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-08-26T03:03:32Z
  • 分类eess.SY, cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

UAV displacement attacks have traditionally relied on spoofing sensors that directly report position or translational motion, such as GNSS and optical flow. In this work, we introduce SonicNudge, a new attack primitive that instead targets the gyroscope and shows that low-level inertial errors can be transformed into controlled displacement of hovering or slow-moving UAVs. The attack exploits estimator--controller coupling: a small gyroscope perturbation by ultrasonic resonance can persist as an attitude-estimation bias, and the flight controller can convert this biased estimate into a shifted hover point. This behavior is especially relevant to UAV tasks that require hovering, station-keeping, slow approach, or precise final alignment, such as perimeter denial, inspection, docking, landing alignment, and close-proximity operation, where meter-scale position errors can be operationally meaningful. We analyze this attack primitive in a PX4-style flight stack and validate it through 81 simulation runs and more than 10 indoor/outdoor physical experiments, showing that displacement is governed by estimator weighting, bias observability, and closed-loop position correction. Our study suggests that UAV and vehicle-system security should look beyond direct navigation spoofing and pay closer attention to low-level inertial errors and estimator--controller coupling as a subtle but important attack surface.
