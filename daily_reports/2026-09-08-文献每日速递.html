VoxelFix: Post-Hoc Semantic Correction of Completed 3D Voxel Maps

  • 作者Sunesh Praveen Raja Sundarasami, Taehyoung Kim, Johannes Scherer, Tomaž Cotič, Sivasubiramaniam Subbiah, Andreas Greiner, Paul Spannaus, Sebastian Houben
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-09-04T13:08:07Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Semantic 3D maps are increasingly constructed automatically for aerial robotics by integrating learned semantic predictions into 3D representations. While this avoids costly manual 3D annotation, errors in the perception and mapping pipeline can persist in the resulting map, reducing its reliability for downstream autonomous tasks. Existing 3D semantic map refinement methods either rely on the original observations, treat occupancy as part of the prediction problem, or apply non-learned local regularization to completed maps. Instead, we study post-hoc semantic correction, asking whether semantic accuracy can be recovered directly from the completed map while keeping its geometry and occupancy fixed. We introduce \method, a graph-based model that corrects voxel labels based on local geometry and neighboring semantic information. To obtain training pairs, we corrupt contiguous regions of annotated OccuFly maps according to class confusions observed in upstream maps. We evaluate \method on completed OccuFly maps generated from predictions of four independently trained 2D segmentation models. \method consistently improves mIoU by 4.23--5.00 percentage points, with gains broadly distributed across the evaluated semantic classes and particularly strong improvements for tree, roof, and wall. Results on an independently reconstructed out-of-distribution aerial scene further suggest that the learned correction can transfer beyond the environments seen during training.

Performance Evaluation of HAPS-enabled Coverage Enhancement in Hard-to-Reach Areas

  • 作者Hao Lin, Mustafa A. Kishk, Mohamed-Slim Alouini
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-09-04T12:27:52Z
  • 分类cs.NI
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

High altitude platform stations (HAPSs) are becoming a key component of future non-terrestrial networks (NTNs). HAPSs can serve a larger area than uncrewed aerial vehicles (UAVs) and offer lower propagation latency, maintenance expense, and energy costs than satellites. A major application of HAPSs is to serve the areas where terrestrial network (TN) deployment is infeasible, especially in hard-to-reach areas and post-disaster areas. For instance, in the Amazon rainforest, the Mediterranean region, and deserts, TN deployment is severely constrained by geographical and environmental conditions. Only areas close to transportation networks or coastlines can be covered, while large areas remain uncovered. Such coverage holes in hard-to-reach areas are typically overlooked in existing literature. Motivated by these realistic cases, in this paper, we use tools from stochastic geometry to mathematically model hard-to-reach areas where cellular terrestrial infrastructure only exists at their perimeter. We propose to deploy a HAPS constellation over this hard-to-reach area to enhance connectivity. For that setup, we derive the downlink (DL) and uplink (UL) coverage performance of the considered user equipment (UE) as a function of the location of the UE inside the coverage hole. Our results show how the number of HAPSs, beamwidth, and HAPS altitude affect the DL and UL coverage probabilities. Finally, we provide multiple useful guidelines for future HAPS deployment.

3D Radar Imaging from the UAV Nadir

  • 作者S. Hamed Javadi, Hichem Sahli, André Bourdoux
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-09-04T08:06:57Z
  • 分类eess.SP
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Radars improve the sensing robustness of UAVs by operating under poor lighting and weather conditions and seeing through occlusions such as vegetation. However, they suffer from poor angular resolution, which can be addressed using synthetic aperture radar (SAR) algorithms. State-of-the-art UAV SAR methods operate at a depression angle and are not suitable for sensor fusion applications where the data are collected from areas directly below the UAV (i.e., the UAV nadir). In this paper, we present an interferometric SAR (InSAR) framework for reconstructing 3D images from the UAV nadir using a low-cost multi-input-multi-output (MIMO) mm-wave radar. Additionally, an effective method based on the phase gradient autofocus (PGA) is presented for compensating the phase error across the virtual receive antennas. We demonstrate the effectiveness of our 3D imaging algorithm in both simulation and experimental scenarios.

空地协同视觉语言导航：基于共享鸟瞰地图的免训练方法

  • 作者Shuning Zhang, Liang Li, Yunheng Wang, Tao Wang, Yihang Kang, Renjing Xu
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-09-03T07:38:41Z
  • 分类cs.RO, cs.AI
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

**研究背景：**
视觉语言导航（VLN）要求智能体依据自然语言指令在真实或仿真环境中自主移动。已有研究多聚焦于单智能体任务，而空地协同设定——无人机拥有全局鸟瞰视角、地面机器人拥有局部第一人称视角——能弥补单一视角的感知局限，具有明显的应用价值，却鲜有探索。现有免训练方法仅能解决单智能体任务，缺乏协同机制。更关键的是，近期的CARLA-Air评测发现，五种先进视觉-语言-动作（VLA）模型在该设定下均无法形成稳定的协作行为；即便是朴素的语义通信或双向耦合策略，也会导致性能下降。这表明如何设计有效的空地协同导航框架，仍是一个尚未解决的开放问题。

**研究思路：**
针对上述挑战，作者提出AGC-VLN，据称是首个面向空地协同视觉语言导航的免训练基线。其核心洞察在于：免训练方法将导航过程解耦为基于VLM的语义推理与确定性的几何执行，从而暴露出一个可交互的协作接口——无人机在全局鸟瞰图中渲染地面机器人上报的位姿、以及由VLM锚定的目标位置，形成带有距离标签的CAR/GOAL标记，构成一张共享鸟瞰地图。地面机器人由此获得第一人称视角无法提供的全局空间上下文，借助冻结的VLM规划沿路行走的路径，并在闭环控制下执行；与此同时，无人机运行升级版空间搜索算法3D-SPF，在俯视视角中定位目标并飞向目标。在CARLA-Air的Town10HD场景中进行100次闭环实验，AGC-VLN达到77.0%的联合成功率，相比性能较弱的单智能体无人机（50.0%）有27.0个百分点的协作增益，并超过先前最强的单智能体基线Travel UAV（53.0%）24.0个百分点。摘要表明，这一增益源自无人机全局视野与地面机器人沿路执行能力之间的互补性。

**局限性：**
摘要中未提供足够信息判断具体局限性。不过从现有描述可推断，实验仅基于CARLA-Air仿真环境中的单一场景（Town10HD）和100次闭环试验，真实世界中的光照、通信延迟、定位噪声等干扰因素尚未涉及；方法依赖VLM的语义推理能力，其在不同语言指令和未见环境下的泛化表现仍需进一步验证。

**值得关注：**
该工作为多智能体具身导航提供了一个清晰、可复现的免训练协同范式：不依赖端到端微调，而是将VLM语义推理与确定性几何控制解耦，以共享鸟瞰地图作为协作接口，规避了多智能体VLA模型协同训练难的瓶颈。这种“感知异构、地图共享、分工执行”的系统设计思路，对无人机-地面机器人协同、自动驾驶车路协同和具身智能多智能体任务均有借鉴意义。此外，其明确的评测指标——联合成功率与协作增益——也为后续空地协同VLN研究提供了可比较的基准。
