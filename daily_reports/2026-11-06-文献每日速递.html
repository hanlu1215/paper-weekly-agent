**研究背景：**
蜂窝连接无人机走廊可复用地面网络基础设施，为空中飞行提供较可预测的连通性；但其与地面用户（GUE）共存时，会在天线倾角和物理资源块（PRB）上形成竞争。现有研究多仅以信号质量为目标优化倾角，未充分处理走廊速率与GUE时延约束下的联合倾角—PRB规划，也未给出资源申请超过PRB预算时的分配策略。论文因此研究在保障无人机走廊最低速率和GUE平均时延上限的同时，最大化GUE和对数速率，具有空地资源协同意义。

**研究思路：**

**研究背景：**
城市管理遥感需要从无人机图像中进行细粒度视觉推理，服务交通监控、城市建设、环境质量和灾害预警等任务。然而，多模态大语言模型（MLLMs）的评测仍较少考察这一能力。现有模型常依赖场景级先验，忽略细粒度视觉证据，难以支撑需要精确判读的城市管理应用。论文由此构建基准，试图检验模型是否真正依据细节推理，对提升遥感MLLM评测可靠性具有重要意义。

**研究思路：**
针对上述不足，作者构建基于无人机图像、面向多类城市管理任务的细粒度基准UMBench，用以评估MLLM的细粒度视觉推理表现。评测发现当前模型偏向场景先验后，进一步提出“证据优先”的思维链方法，以免训练方式引导模型先检查细粒度视觉证据，再完成推理。摘要称实验在开源与商用MLLM上均取得性能提升，表明该证据引导策略有效；具体实验设置摘要未说明。

**局限性：**
摘要未提供UMBench的数据规模、任务划分、评价指标、基线配置及统计显著性等信息，难以判断基准覆盖是否充分、是否存在数据偏差。所提方法为免训练，效果可能受不同MLLM自身能力与提示设计影响；真实部署、跨区域与跨传感器泛化及鲁棒性仍需验证。摘要中未提供足够信息判断具体局限性。

**值得关注：**
该工作将“细粒度证据优先”引入遥感MLLM评测与推理，对机器人、具身智能和自动驾驶中的无人机感知、场景理解与可解释决策有借鉴价值；其任务设定和免训练思维链可作为可迁移的评测与提示策略，后续可关注证据定位能否量化、跨模型泛化及真实系统验证。

何时求助：不确定性触发的地空协同越野导航细化

  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-04T16:02:59Z
  • 分类cs.RO, eess.SY
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

**研究背景：**
异质地空团队可利用无人机空中感知弥补地面车辆传感器范围与视角的不足，但何时请求协助、应采集哪类信息仍是难点。现有方法多依赖固定支援角色、任务结构或单纯不确定性，无法判断“消除不确定性是否真能改善地面导航”，因而易出现无效飞行或援助不足。论文聚焦这一决策问题。

**研究思路：**
作者提出以规划器为条件的地空协助方法：先评估地图不确定性对UGV当前MPPI轨迹滚动预测的影响；若导航仍受损，则在解析后的地图假设下重新为同一批轨迹打分，选出带来最大有效提升的地图层，请求对应空中测量并融合、重规划。仿真在三条越野路线的180次试验中达到98.9%目标达成率，UAV平均飞行时间较持续贪婪空中建图减少87.7%；每1秒估计飞行时间可节省2.94秒地面导航时间，约2 km实车实验也展示了不确定性消解与导航恢复。

**局限性：**
摘要未说明地图与传感器噪声建模、失败案例及与更多基线的比较；实车实验规模约2 km，路线与场景多样性、跨环境泛化仍待验证。方法依赖MPPI滚动预测与地图假设生成，其计算开销及假设质量的影响摘要未说明。

**值得关注：**
该工作把“是否求助”转化为对下游规划性能的可量化收益评估，为具身智能中的主动感知与异构协同提供了可借鉴准则；用同一批轨迹在不确定与解析假设下对比打分的思路，可迁移到其他“按需调用外部感知”的任务。

Uncertainty-Driven Efficient Communication for UAV-Based Collaborative Perception

  • 作者Jiuwu Hao, Yuting Wan, Liguo Sun, Zao Zhang, Yueyang Wu, Pin Lv
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-04T15:16:57Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV)-based collaborative perception enables multiple agents to share complementary perceptual information, overcoming limitations such as occlusion and limited sensing range. Communication efficiency is a critical bottleneck, as UAVs operate under stringent bandwidth and computational constraints. However, existing spatial selection methods focus predominantly on foreground regions to reduce missed detections, neglecting perceptually ambiguous regions and leaving false detections unresolved. We propose UCP, an uncertainty-driven framework that characterizes collaborative value through observational uncertainty, identifying sensor-limited regions, and discriminative uncertainty, capturing model-level ambiguity near the decision boundary. These estimates drive spatial request and supply maps for efficient inter-agent communication, and the received messages are aggregated via U-Fusion, an uncertainty-guided cross-attention module. Experiments on three benchmarks across 3D object detection and trajectory prediction demonstrate a superior performance-bandwidth trade-off with high interpretability and strong generalizability.

3D Pose Estimation in Projection Space via Pixel-level Visible Light Communication

  • 作者Michikuni Eguchi, Takefumi Hiraki
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-04T08:07:10Z
  • 分类cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Projector-based augmented reality enables natural interaction between users and devices by overlaying digital information onto the physical world through projected imagery. A key technical requirement of such systems is accurate positional alignment between the projector and the devices. Pixel-level Visible Light Communication (PVLC) achieves this alignment without external sensing infrastructure by embedding invisible spatial codes into each projected pixel through high-speed brightness modulation that preserves image quality. However, the information that PVLC transmits is inherently planar. PVLC has therefore been limited to devices moving on a flat surface and cannot estimate the 3D pose of devices in three-dimensional space, such as aerial robots. To extend PVLC-based pose estimation to three dimensions, we formulate it as a Perspective-n-Point (PnP) problem. The 2D pixel positions that four coplanar photosensors on the device receive via PVLC serve as the image points, and their known 3D layout provides the object points. Experiments show that the proposed system estimates 3D pose with a latency of approximately 8~ms and a 3D position RMSE of 19.2~mm after Kalman filtering. These results confirm the first realization of real-time 3D pose estimation coordinated with high-quality projected image content without external sensing infrastructure.

High-fidelity training-free photovoltaic module mapping from multi-view UAV imagery

  • 作者WenHao Duan, Qi Chen, Penglei Zhang, Ruizhe Deng, Zhiling Guo, Haoran Zhang, Yuntian Chen
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-03T16:14:16Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

High-fidelity photovoltaic (PV) module mapping requires complete instance recovery and accurate delineation. Vision foundation models offer zero-shot segmentation, but converting incomplete and inconsistent predictions across views into a complete, geometrically accurate map of individual modules remains challenging. We propose the Multi-View PV Module Mapper (MV-PVMapper), a training-free framework coupling vision foundation models with unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) photogrammetry. First, Segment Anything Model 3 (SAM 3) generates candidates from original images using text prompts. Their contours are then transferred into object space through per-module slope correction to limit distortion from surface-model noise, and verified against representative module dimensions to guide cross-view instance selection. Finally, accepted instances return to all covering images as box prompts for SAM~3, supplementing text prompts with spatial cues for iterative segmentation, verification and fusion. On five-direction surveys of three operational rooftop PV plants at approximately 1 cm nadir ground sampling distance, two feedback iterations recover 7,710 of 7,711 reference modules with no false positives at an intersection-over-union (IoU) matching threshold of 0.5. Macro-averaged area precision and recall reach 99.00 % and 98.52 %, respectively. Averaged across sites, the aggregated Jaccard index reaches 97.55 %, compared with 60.73 % and 41.24 % for orthomosaic segmentation and multi-view pixel-voting baselines, respectively; average precision at the stricter IoU threshold of 0.95 reaches 95.67 %. Ablation studies show that module-size constraints are critical for instance separation, while slope correction and feedback to all covering views improve boundary accuracy. These results are achieved without task-specific image annotation or segmentation-model weight updates.

**研究背景：**

**局限性：**
摘要未提供足够信息判断具体局限性。可谨慎关注：原型维护与逐层位置校准可能带来计算和存储开销；方法在严重遮挡、类别不平衡及跨数据集泛化下的表现尚需更多验证；真实系统部署效果摘要未说明。

**值得关注：**
该工作将层级位置校准与视图特定原型对比结合，对无人机—地面协同感知、机器人跨视角行人检索及多视角具身感知具有借鉴意义；其Rank-1/mAP评价与双原型设计也值得后续跨域重识别研究参考。

Mass Scaling Laws for Energy-Optimal Horizontal Multirotor Flight

  • 作者Ayush Patnaik, Nicolas Michel, Leonard Baursfeld, Davide Scaramuzza, Xinfan Lin
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-03T12:02:36Z
  • 分类cs.RO, eess.SY
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Multicopters are a type of aerial robot seeing extensive real-world applications but with range and endurance as critical bottlenecks. Optimization of its energy efficiency through energy-aware flight planning (e.g. cruise speed-scheduling) has hence been an important topic. The mass of the multicopter is an important factor to consider, as it has a significant impact on efficiency, and many common applications are subject to frequent mass variation due to payload change. This work derives closed-form scaling relationships between the energy-optimal steady horizontal flight behavior of a multicopter and its vehicle mass, and validates them experimentally on two multirotor platforms. Specifically, for mass-only variation, i.e., when payload changes the vehicle mass while the airframe geometry, aerodynamic characteristics, and rotor configuration remain unchanged, we show through a combination of numerical simulation, theoretical derivation, and experimental validation that (1) the energy-optimal velocity is proportional to the square root of vehicle mass; (2) the energy-optimal pitch angle is constant with respect to mass; and (3) the optimal energy per meter is proportional to mass. The analysis shows that these three relationships arise from a common mass-similarity property of the steady-state flight and rotor-aerodynamic equations. Furthermore, the obtained scaling relationships enable simple yet effective cruise-speed planning to optimize energy efficiency and extend the range and endurance of the multicopter.

**研究背景：** 小型多旋翼无人机具有特征性声学信号，使麦克风成为一种低成本检测手段。然而，公开无人机音频基准上报告的高准确率常依赖随机片段级划分：数据集由少数长录音切分并与现有声库拼接而成，同一来源片段可能同时出现在训练与测试中，精度因而被高估。如何在来源互不重叠的严格评估下有效融合多视图特征，是该任务尚未充分解决的问题。

**研究思路：** 作者在 DroneAudioDataset（11,704 条 1 秒片段）上构建按来源分组的五折三重复协议，并与随机划分对照。每条片段用 57 个手工声学描述子（经 mRMR+ReliefF 混合选择或 NSGA-II 筛选）和 YAMNet 的 1024 维嵌入两个视图表征，分别以随机森林、XGBoost 学习后堆叠集成。分组协议下平均准确率 95.29%、特异度 98.29%、AUC 0.990，98.78% 未见 ESC-50 源片段被正确判为非无人机；随机划分高估 1.89–3.22 个百分点；加入嵌入提升 1.11 个百分点；16–18 个特征即可匹配 44 个基线描述子；单线程每片段 51.0 ms。

**局限性：** 摘要未说明跨背景噪声、真实飞行距离及多机场景的泛化能力，实验仅涉及单一公开数据集与闭集二分类；嵌入只比较了 YAMNet，未与其他预训练模型对照；51.0 ms 为单 CPU 线程结果，摘要未提供嵌入式或端侧实测。

This paper considers online intrusion-risk assessment and graded warning for unmanned aerial vehicles under bounded measurement attacks. To address this problem, we develop a monitoring framework that combines trusted state estimation, attack-aware risk assessment, and probabilistic signal temporal logic (PrSTL). Trusted observations support recursive state estimation, while potentially attacked observations inform integrity testing and intrusion-risk assessment. The key contribution is a confidence-set construction that combines normalized innovation statistics with physical attack bounds to characterize admissible measurement biases. The resulting family of bias-corrected Gaussian posteriors yields an upper bound on the probability of occupying a no-fly zone or its surrounding safety margin. Confidence guarantees and finite-cover numerical certificates quantify the reliability of this risk envelope, which is evaluated with or without an integrity alarm. The PrSTL monitor analyzes the resulting risk sequence to distinguish ordered, persistent, and repeated risk and produces graded warnings with explicit robustness margins and supporting windows. We validate the monitor in flight experiments with a Crazyflie quadrotor tracked by a Vicon motion capture system. Controlled comparisons quantify the contributions of individual monitoring components.   • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-02T19:53:10Z
  • 分类cs.IT, eess.SP, eess.SY

We consider a UAV-assisted status-update system in which a rotary-wing unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) collects updates from multiple sensors, stores the newest update of each sensor, and forwards selected buffered updates to a ground station (GS). We minimize a weighted sum of the time-average GS-side age of information (AoI) and UAV energy consumption over a finite horizon. The optimization is subject to half-duplex operation, sensor and UAV energy-causality constraints, transmission-rate and power limits, and a terminal-location constraint. Because collection changes the information age at the UAV but does not reduce the GS-side AoI until the buffered update is forwarded, mobility, collection, and forwarding decisions are coupled over time. We use a hierarchical AoI-energy actor-critic (HAE-AC) controller in which a coarse-timescale actor selects return-feasible UAV movements, while a deterministic per-slot scheduler constructs feasible collection or forwarding sets and assigns the minimum transmit power required for each scheduled transmission. In a 100-sensor deployment, HAE-AC reduces the average GS-side AoI by about 22% relative to an adaptive staleness-gated benchmark. Compared with forward-upon-collection, selective buffered forwarding reduces the number of downlink bursts by roughly 30% while increasing the number of collections per sensor by more than 23%.

This paper proposes an artificial intelligence (AI) driven mission capability aware unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) swarm coordination framework for autonomous post disaster search and rescue. An integrated sensing and communication enabled master high altitude platform station senses the disaster environment, detects victims and hazards, and constructs adaptive rescue regions based on spatially varying mission demands. A mission capability requirement (MCR) quantifies regional capability demand, while a normalized mission capability score (MCS) characterizes the real time capability of individual UAVs. A capability-aware assignment mechanism matches UAV capabilities to regional requirements while minimizing deployment distance. An MCS based reallocation mechanism dynamically transfers UAVs from capability surplus to capability deficient regions while preserving donor sufficiency. A hierarchical deep reinforcement learning framework with proximal policy optimization performs high-level region selection, while MCR MCS matching enables feasible UAV level reallocation. Finally, joint trajectory optimization considers travel time, distance, propulsion energy, operational capability, obstacle avoidance, and inter UAV collision avoidance. Theoretical analysis and simulations demonstrate improved mission capability satisfaction, resource efficiency, and disaster response performance
