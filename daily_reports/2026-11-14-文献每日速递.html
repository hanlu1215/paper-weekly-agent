Consensus Capture in Reference-Based UAV Video Stabilization: A Benchmark on Dynamic Urban Scenes

  • 作者Robert Fonod, Nikolas Geroliminis
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-12T17:55:06Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Reference-frame stabilization registers every frame of a hovering-drone video onto one reference frame, keeping object positions comparable. The registration pipelines it relies on are selected on feature-matching benchmarks in which only the camera moves. Over a busy intersection, vehicles moving together supply mutually consistent correspondences that can draw the robust homography estimate toward their motion: consensus capture, which inlier statistics cannot flag. A two-part benchmark measures it and the object masking meant to prevent it. A static protocol scores ten pipelines under exactly known homographies on 4K aerial frames, tuned on a site-disjoint split at a matched resolution and feature budget. A dynamic protocol scores them at an offline and a real-time budget on hovers selected from the video alone to span the amount and coherence of moving foreground, plus a stationary control. It measures background alignment, object position error and the share of a moving group's displacement an estimate follows, against a pseudo-ground-truth reference audited with manually annotated ground control points, which also score every candidate. On static scenes, tuned AKAZE, RootSIFT, SIFT and BRISK lead, retuning the matcher thresholds raises the mean average accuracy (mAA) by at most 0.0062, and masking costs less than 0.005. Unmasked ORB is captured on 28 of 304 real-time dynamic pairs and on 6 offline; its real-time captured estimates have a median corner error of 18.8 px yet typical inlier counts and ratios. Masking removes capture for every pipeline and leaves the leading pipelines unchanged. Code, data and results are released.

UAV-Flow++: A Unified Suite for Benchmarking and Learning Language-Guided UAV Flight

  • 作者Donglin Yang, Xiangyu Wang, Yantong Zhong, Wenhao Zheng, Zihan Ding, Yue Liao, Hongsheng Li, Ke Xu, Si Liu
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-12T17:39:43Z
  • 分类cs.RO, cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Language-guided UAV navigation has largely focused on long-horizon route planning, leaving short-range, object-relative flight maneuvers underexplored. To address this gap, we formulate Flying-on-a-Word (Flow), a task requiring a UAV to convert concise natural-language instruction, egocentric visual observation, and current state dynamics into continuous flight actions. Flow encompasses both primitive motion commands and complex object-interactive behaviors, assessing motion intent comprehension and spatial context grounding. To enable policy learning and closed-loop evaluation, we introduce UAV-Flow, a comprehensive dataset comprising 30,692 expert-piloted real-world trajectories across eight distinct motion categories, alongside 10,109 simulation trajectories in the UAV-Flow-Sim environment equipped with task-specific completion criteria. Furthermore, we develop FlowVLA, which undergoes imitation pretraining on mixed real-sim demonstrations and online post-training via Tail-Aware GRPO (TGRPO). By leveraging group reward distributions, TGRPO effectively reinforces high-reward action sequences. Evaluated on 273 held-out UAV-Flow-Sim episodes, pretrained FlowVLA achieves a mean success rate of 75.58%, outperforming the strongest baseline by 6.72 percentage points, which TGRPO post-training further elevates to 93.16% with superior sample efficiency. Physical UAV experiments demonstrate robust execution of object-relative commands in both indoor and outdoor real-world scenes. Together, Flow establishes a novel benchmark and learning framework for language-conditioned flight maneuvers.

A Class of Bayesian Conjugate Distributions on $SE_n(3)$ for Motion Estimation

  • 作者Shijie Wang, Haichao Gui, Rui Zhong
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-12T13:58:05Z
  • 分类stat.ME, cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

This paper proposes a novel class of Bayesian Conjugate Distributions (BCDs) on extended special Euclidean groups SE_n(3) for global motion uncertainty representation and estimation. BCDs are constructed by restricting matrix Gaussian distributions on ambient Euclidean space to SE_n(3), avoiding singularity of local coordinates. The unique feature of BCDs is their conjugacy to a broad class of sampling distributions arising in motion estimation, enabling exact closed-form Bayesian inference. Optimal estimation with respect to BCDs is further investigated for implementing motion estimation. Two means are extracted as random state estimates, which maximize probability and minimize mean squared error, respectively. Moreover, within the BCD class, moment matching is shown to minimize the Kullback-Leibler divergence, and maximum likelihood estimation is also derived, which provide analytical methods to approximate underlying distributions with the optimality guarantee. As an application, a Bayesian estimator for pose and velocity estimation of an Unmanned Aerial Vehicle (UAV) is developed within an assumed density filtering architecture. The propagation and estimate extraction leverage the optimal estimation related to BCDs, and the landmark measurements are exactly fused with the BCD prior in closed form due to the conjugacy. Numerical simulations of a UAV flight indicate the advantages of BCD-based estimators over two classic Kalman filters on Lie groups with various initial uncertainties and measurement noise.

Measurement-Calibrated Synthetic Pretraining for Rapid Adaptation of UAV Channel Predictors

  • 作者Qiuheng Zhou, Wei Jiang, Donglin Wang, Hans D. Schotten
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-12T11:19:06Z
  • 分类eess.SP, cs.LG
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) links can change faster than route-specific channel data can be collected, so a channel predictor must often be adapted from only a short target-flight calibration segment. This paper studies measurement-calibrated synthetic pretraining as an offline source prior for rapid adaptation of channel state information (CSI) predictors. The synthetic source is calibrated to flight-only power evolution, temporal shape variation, frequency selectivity, and phase disturbance, and is then combined with measured source-flight fine-tuning before target-flight adaptation. Under chronological held-out flight splits, synthetic-to-real (S2R) fine-tuning reduces the measured-data budget needed to reach useful prediction in the low-data regime. For 50 ms-ahead prediction, it crosses a latest-channel-estimate persistence baseline with 50 measured training windows, roughly 0.5 s of 10 ms-spaced supervised windows, whereas training from scratch requires 600 windows. A target-prefix adaptation study further shows that a lightweight gated recurrent unit predictor can reuse the pretrained source prior with one prediction-layer update epoch, positioning measurement-calibrated synthetic pretraining as a reusable prior for scarce target-flight measurements.

Empowering AI towards 6G: Realistic UAV Channel Data Acquisition using Open-Source Solutions

  • 作者Qiuheng Zhou, Sergiy Melnyk, Hans D. Schotten, Robert Vilter, Nick Stuckert
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-12T11:18:49Z
  • 分类eess.SP
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

The "sim-to-real" gap introduced by hardware impairments motivates realistic experimental platforms for AI-native research in Non-Terrestrial Networks (NTN). This paper presents an open-source UAV-to-Ground measurement testbed built on the OpenAirInterface (OAI) stack and commodity Software Defined Radios (SDRs), enabling direct extraction of raw Sub-6 GHz Channel State Information (CSI) synchronized with flight telemetry. Through real-world flight campaigns, we observe a velocity-bandwidth decoupling effect: under unguided, measurement-only ridge extraction, dominant spectral ridges deviate strongly from the theoretical kinematic Doppler trajectory, whereas guided tracking substantially reduces this mismatch. This indicates that Doppler information is present, but not dominant, in the raw observable dynamics of commodity SDR-based measurements. We further benchmark representative deep learning predictors on this hardware-inclusive dataset and show, through a case study, that their behavior differs substantially from idealized expectations. By open-sourcing the testbed and dataset, we provide a realistic foundation for training and evaluating AI models for practical aerial network deployments.

**研究背景：**

**研究思路：**

**局限性：**
摘要未说明数据来源覆盖范围、标注一致性及跨传感器泛化能力，也未给出各子场景的误差分析；评测仅涉及 15 个 VLM，RelCap-F1 与解析器的鲁棒性仍需验证。摘要中未提供足够信息判断具体局限性。

**值得关注：**
该工作把“定位—属性—空间关系”纳入统一评分，为具身智能和机器人遥感感知提供了可借鉴的任务设定与评价思路；有向框与关系元组可支撑无人机巡检、灾害评估等需要可核验空间描述的应用。其结论提示后续模型应优先补强实例定位，再提升关系描述的可解释性。

**研究背景：**
UAV覆盖分析常按LoS/NLoS概率在分贝域加权平均路径损耗，但条件路径损耗呈双峰分布，平均值并不对应任何真实链路。既有做法可能因此系统性扭曲覆盖高度与中断评估，论文旨在给出该平均误差的闭式表达并量化其影响。

**研究思路：**
作者从双峰路径损耗导致“均值无物理链路”的问题出发，推导分贝域平均路径损耗相对“平均接收功率对应路径损耗”的误差闭式解；随后对ITU-R P.1410城市做射线追踪，并输入精确LoS概率以排除参数误差，比较链路误差、覆盖最优高度和中断概率。结果显示平均路径损耗高6.6 dB，仅6.2%链路落在其2 dB内；最优高度由1764 m变为778 m，中断概率最多低估0.198。

**局限性：**
验证主要依赖单一ITU-R城市场景的射线追踪，摘要未说明是否包含真实测量或多场景泛化；结论对传播模型、城市形态和LoS概率假设的敏感性仍需检验，也缺少真实无人机系统验证。摘要中未提供足够信息判断其计算复杂度与适用边界。

**值得关注：**
该工作提醒具身智能与空中机器人覆盖规划：不要直接把分贝域加权均值当作可观测链路质量，应区分“平均功率”和“平均路径损耗”，并在高度优化、断连风险评估中校验双峰分布影响；其闭式误差和射线追踪评价方式可迁移到UAV通信规划与仿真基准。

**研究背景：**

RSLiveBench-UAV: A Continually Evolving Vision-Language Benchmark for Aerial Spatial Intelligence

  • 作者Yue Zhou, Jiawei Liu, Jue Chen, Ziyang Gong, Haojia Wei, Jingxiang Li, Xue Jiang, Zhihang Zhong, Pai Peng, Xue Yang, Yansheng Li, Jonathan Li
  • 来源arXiv

  • 论文链接

Aerial spatial intelligence (ASI) requires models to understand objects and spatial relationships across changing viewpoints and scales. Evaluating these abilities as models evolve calls for fresh physical observations, yet producing new aerial spatial questions requires repeatedly establishing entity identities, geometric references, and cross-view correspondences. We introduce \textbf{RSLiveBench-UAV}, a continually evolving vision-language benchmark driven by recurring acquisition of real UAV imagery. Its update protocol admits images captured after the preceding release and delays answer disclosure. An entity-centered production workflow uses geometry-assisted association to link repeated observations of physical vehicles, reuse human-verified attributes, and combine them with view-specific annotations and spatial references for traceable question generation. All tasks use deterministic scoring without model judges. We evaluate 30 general-purpose, remote-sensing, and embodied models on 35,491 question records from ten scenes, covering 17 tasks across 2D perception, 3D perception, cross-view perception, and reasoning. All 18 tested embodied models outperform the three selected remote-sensing comparators in Overall, although the strongest embodied model scores 43.29 versus 59.88 for GPT-6-Astra. Fine-grained vehicle recognition remains challenging, and performance varies across heights and camera pitches. Retrospective scene-group comparisons and rolling-window replay characterize score-pattern consistency under scene renewal. RSLiveBench-UAV connects fresh physical observations, entity-centered answer production, and systematic model evaluation to support continual assessment of ASI.
