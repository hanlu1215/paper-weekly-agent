**研究背景：**
多无人机协同执行路径跟随时，既需要各机收敛到参考路径，又需要在路径上保持均匀间距并避免相互碰撞，这对分布式自主引导提出了较高要求。摘要指出，现有研究路线在这两方面的综合保障仍不充分，因此论文聚焦于去中心化、无碰撞的路径跟随引导问题，并希望为多机沿路径的均匀编队提供可分析的理论保证。

**研究思路：**
针对多机收敛过程中的碰撞风险与间距不均问题，作者采用矢量场引导律驱动每架无人机趋向参考路径；利用无人机间的相对距离与方位设计旋转排斥机制，用于收敛阶段的避碰；同时提出基于机间间距误差的速度控制律，以实现沿路径的均匀分离。论文对避碰和间距误差收敛到零给出了解析保证，并通过数值仿真验证方法有效性。

**局限性：**
摘要仅提及数值仿真，未说明是否包含真实飞行实验、复杂风扰与通信约束下的表现，也未给出规模扩展性与参数敏感性分析；理论保证所依赖的模型假设在摘要中未展开，具体局限仍需全文进一步确认。

**值得关注：**
该工作将矢量场引导、相对方位排斥与间距误差反馈结合，为多机编队的分布式引导提供了可分析的避碰与一致性思路，对多机器人协同路径跟随、编队保持及后续真实系统部署具有参考价值。

Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) extend embodied intelligence into continuous three-dimensional space, where perception, reasoning, physical embodiment, and action are tightly coupled through flight and environmental interaction. Recent advances in foundation models, world models, and AI agents are shifting UAV autonomy from task-specific perception and control toward systems that can interpret human intent, understand open environments, reason about physical consequences, and organize complex behaviors under embodiment and flight-dynamic constraints. We characterize this emerging paradigm as UAV embodied intelligence (UAV EI) and distinguish it from its system realization, the embodied-intelligent UAV (EI UAV). To provide a unified view of the field, we introduce a 5+5 framework that describes UAV EI through five capability dimensions and EI UAVs through five architectural layers spanning physical embodiment, general cognition, embodied skills, external interaction, and system harnessing. Based on this framework, we systematically review recent progress in embodied morphology, embodied perception, world models, embodied planning, vision-language navigation, embodied manipulation, and embodied collaboration. We further identify long-horizon autonomy, predictive physical reasoning, test-time skill acquisition, and autonomous capability evolution as key challenges toward more general aerial embodied intelligence. Finally, we argue that harnessing physical-digital AI agents, through persistent coupling of digital intelligence with physical sensing, dynamics, action, and feedback, provides a system-level pathway toward adaptive and continuously evolving UAV autonomy. **研究背景：**
单目无人机视频可为复杂城市场景的动态重建提供重要观测，但此类场景具有显著的时空异质性：不同区域的活动模式不同，部分动态区域的运动状态还会随时间演化。现有基于分解共享时空特征场的动态高斯方法，虽在目标中心或较紧凑场景中实现了高效、准确的重建，但其常用的固定平面特征组合机制难以适应无人机场景的局部动态差异，容易产生重影伪影和动态细节模糊。因此，论文关注的核心问题是：如何让不同局部单元根据自身空间与时间状态，获得自适应的动态特征表示。

**研究思路：**
针对上述问题，作者提出自适应锚点变形框架AdaAnchor4D。方法上，Anchor-Conditioned Feature Aggregation（ACFA）利用锚点特定的聚合嵌入和时间信息，自适应聚合共享时空特征；Decoupled Local Geometry Deformation（DLGD）将锚点状态变形与局部高斯几何变形解耦；Density-Adaptive Coordinate Warping（DACW）依据轴向上的锚点分布重参数化特征查询坐标，以缓解非均匀几何采样与均匀网格参数化之间的失配。论文在UAV-Arc4D、VisDrone和UAVDT上验证，摘要称其渲染质量高于代表性动态高斯方法，同时保持实时渲染性能。

**局限性：**
摘要未提供足够信息判断具体局限性。实验仅在三个无人机数据集上验证，摘要未说明实时性能对应的平台、分辨率与场景规模，也未说明方法对严重遮挡、剧烈运动、光照变化及跨场景泛化的鲁棒性；真实无人机系统部署效果仍需进一步验证。代码将公开，但摘要未给出实现细节与失败案例分析。

**值得关注：**
该工作将锚点条件特征聚合、局部几何变形解耦和密度自适应坐标变换引入单目无人机动态重建，对机器人/具身智能中的动态场景感知、无人机自主导航与动态SLAM具有借鉴意义。其强调局部时空自适应与实时渲染的联合评价，也提示后续研究关注非均匀采样建模和真实部署效率。

Knowing the Self, Understanding the World: A Dual-Cognition Benchmark for UAV Spatio-temporal Reasoning with MLLMs

Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) perform strongly across vision-language tasks, yet remain insufficiently explored in UAV scenarios. Recent UAV-oriented benchmarks have begun to evaluate MLLMs in aerial scenarios, but typically focus on scene understanding, event recognition, or navigation completion, rather than jointly assessing the dual-cognition capability required for UAV agents: reasoning about the UAV's own position, behavior, and observation state alongside external landmark states under continuously changing viewpoints, supported by explicit spatio-temporal grounding. To address this gap, we present UAV-DualCog, the first benchmark to formulate UAV dual cognition as the coordinated evaluation of Agent-State Reasoning and World-State Reasoning across multi-view images and egocentric videos, with explicit spatial or temporal grounding. The State-aware UAV Simulation Platform (SUSP) and the DualCog-aware Capture System (DCCS) construct 4,096 image and 2,048 video samples from 12 scenes, pairing complementary state questions within shared Agent-Landmark contexts. Extensive evaluations on 45 models show that current MLLMs remain far from reliable in UAV dual cognition: Agent-State Reasoning is generally weaker than World-State Reasoning, semantic answers do not reliably coincide with spatial or temporal grounding, and coordinated abilities remain limited. Thinking-mode comparisons, open-ended analyses, and sim-to-real validation show that these limitations persist across inference settings, formats, and environments. We further construct UAV-DualCog-Train and improve UAV dual-cognition performance through a lightweight optimization probe. Supplementary website, code, and dataset: https://smartdianlab.github.io/UAV-DualCog/

**研究背景：**
高分辨率影像中的实时微小目标感知对具身搜救任务至关重要，但无人机等边缘设备受严格尺寸、重量与功耗约束。传统图像下采样会造成严重特征损失，切片式处理又带来难以接受的高延迟，因而亟需在SWaP受限条件下直接处理4K影像的感知方案。

**研究思路：**
针对上述瓶颈，作者提出涵盖新架构、专用数据集与硬件级基准的综合框架。MITE-Net采用级联结构，将生物启发、无需学习的微小目标运动区域提议网络TTM-RPN与不足0.14M参数的R-CNN类检测头结合；并通过重标注两个无人机数据集构建SAR-Tiny。在Jetson AGX Xavier上对比YOLO后，海上4K场景达到100%搜索成功率、30.33 FPS、3.19 W和9.51 FPS/W，召回与能效优于基线；城市场景则暴露前端与轻量头的复合结构限制。

**局限性：**
方法在动态海上场景表现突出，但在杂乱城市场景中失败，说明泛化能力受背景复杂度和超轻检测头表征能力制约；摘要仅给出边缘设备基准结果，真实SAR全流程、多场景鲁棒性及数据集偏差仍需进一步验证。

**值得关注：**
其“无学习运动先验＋超轻检测头”的SWaP设计、4K直接处理思路、重标注数据集及FPS/W能效评价，对具身边缘感知和无人机搜救有借鉴价值；城市失败也提示未来需加强复杂背景下的前端—检测头协同设计。

A Review of Vision-Based Vehicle Detection for UAV-Based Traffic Monitoring: Experimental Insights and Future Directions

  • 作者{'fullname': 'Jianlin Ye', 'username': ''}, {'fullname': 'Christos Kyrkou', 'username': ''}
  • 来源OpenReview
  • 发布时间2026-12-31T00:00:00Z
  • 分类CoRR 2026
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

In Intelligent Transportation System (ITS), unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV)-based surveillance offers an innovative solution to traffic surveillance with wide coverage and real-time data collection capabilities. In comparison to fixed ground-based infrastructure, UAVs are able to respond to dynamic traffic but present challenges such as vehicle detection at varying altitudes, compensation for motion-induced image variations and efficient processing of high-resolution images. Deep learning has been largely beneficial on improving the detection accuracy; however, for practical deployment, a critical assessment of the accuracy, latency, and harmonization with current transportation systems needs to be carefully considered. This survey reviews recent advancements in the UAV-based traffic monitoring, with a primary focus being deep neural network models for traffic analytics in various urban settings. Three main challenges identified in the literature are ensuring compatibility with traffic control systems, achieving real-time processing to optimize traffic flow, and maintaining robust detection in different environmental conditions. Existing solutions often lack comprehensive frameworks for utilizing UAV captured data to respond to incidents and manage traffic effectively. Future research should focus on optimal detection models, edge processing, and adaptive control integration to improve the responsiveness of urban traffic management.
