5G ISAC-Based UAV Detection and 3-D Tracking Using Uplink Sounding Reference Signals on an End-to-End O-RAN Simulation Testbed

  • 作者Arun K. Gurung, Satha K. Sathananthan, Shiva R. Pokhrel
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-08-06T09:56:00Z
  • 分类cs.NI
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC) lets cellular infrastructure serve communication users and sense on the same waveform. We present an end-to-end O-RAN simulation testbed for 5G ISAC targeting low-altitude UAV detection and 3-D tracking, built from open-source components: OpenAirInterface, FlexRIC and Sionna RT, in which the NR Uplink Sounding Reference Signal is repurposed as a passive radar waveform: a PHY-layer sensing stage inside the gNB produces detections that reach an Extended Kalman Filter tracking xApp over a custom E2 service model, with no change to the NR standard and no dedicated sensing waveform. A single bistatic pair leaves elevation unobservable, so the tracker needs a height prior; we remove it two independent ways and measure both - a planar receive array supplying a vertical aperture, and a second transmitter supplying range diversity. Both live results corroborate an offline ray-traced study of the same estimator, which converges from a deliberately wrong initial altitude to 1.8 m RMSE at a consistent filter, so altitude observability is established both in the signal-processing chain and end to end through the live stack. Detection coverage is preserved under a concurrent 10 Mbps uplink communications load.

**研究背景：**
通感一体化（ISAC）正从面向功能的物理层技术，向目标驱动、闭环可控的智能系统演进。现有研究在基于学习的感知、资源分配、智能反射面、边缘智能、多智能体协同及韧性网络等方面大多彼此孤立，缺乏统一视角。论文提出AISAC范式，旨在将智能体AI（Agentic AI）与ISAC深度融合，解决当前技术碎片化、缺乏目标导向闭环的问题，并系统梳理该交叉领域的研究进展与不足。

**研究思路：**
针对现有研究分散、缺乏整体框架的问题，作者提出一个六阶段闭环框架，涵盖观察、情境化、推理与预测、规划与编排、执行与协作、反馈与韧性，并引入五级智能体成熟度模型。该框架统一了多模态智能、大语言模型、强化学习、联邦学习、RIS辅助控制、无人机与车载网络及AI原生网络管理等方向的文献。论文通过对代表性研究进行九项智能体特定评估标准的审计，发现尚无系统能报告超过一两项标准，暴露出宣称与展示的智能体成熟度之间存在差距，并据此指出物理到语义对齐、预测世界模型、实时智能体-物理层交互等开放挑战。

**局限性：**
摘要未提供具体实验或系统验证细节，难以判断框架在真实场景中的有效性；审计样本的范围、评估标准的具体实施方式以及所涉系统是否覆盖全部ISAC场景，摘要均未说明。因此，该工作作为综述更多提供概念性统一与趋势判断，其实际工程可落地性仍需后续研究验证。

**值得关注：**
该论文为具身智能与机器人系统中的感知-推理-行动闭环设计提供了可借鉴的建模思路，尤其是六阶段闭环与智能体成熟度分级可用于评估自主系统的智能化水平。多智能体协同、资源高效自主和跨层交互等开放问题，也提示未来研究需更重视“宣称能力”与“可验证能力”之间的一致性评价，推动AI系统向真正目标驱动的闭环演进。

Iterative Hybrid Discrete-Continuous Viewpoint Planning for UAV Photogrammetry

  • 作者Alan Grech, Daniel Pisani, Andre Grima, Carl James Debono, Saviour Formosa, Dylan Seychell
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-08-06T07:57:33Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) photogrammetry requires camera networks that provide sufficient surface coverage, image overlap, parallax, and resolution, yet conventional flight patterns are often poorly adapted to scene geometry resulting in local reconstruction errors. This paper proposes an iterative hybrid discrete-continuous viewpoint planning method for targeted UAV photogrammetry from a proxy reconstruction. The method scores sampled surface points using photogrammetric heuristics based on frontality, imaging distance, parallax, and multi-view observation count, while also evaluating the full viewpoint set in terms of visibility, pairwise overlap, and graph connectivity. Candidate viewpoints are generated around weakly observed regions, refined using clustered Covariance matrix adaptation evolution strategy (CMA-ES) optimisation, and removed when redundant. The final flight path combines close-range detail viewpoints with wider model-coverage viewpoints, balancing local reconstruction quality with global image-network robustness. Evaluation on three synthetic scenes shows that the proposed method improves both reconstruction accuracy and completeness compared with prior UAV path-planning methods.

**研究背景：**
航空图像目标导航要求无人机根据目标图像自主到达指定位置。现有基于世界模型的方法通过预测未来状态对候选轨迹排序，但通常仅依赖单一或少量点预测，难以应对大规模户外环境中显著的状态不确定性，限制了导航鲁棒性。为此，本文提出不确定性感知导航世界模型（UA-NWM），旨在解决不确定性条件下可靠导航决策的核心问题，对机器人在复杂开放环境中实现目标导航具有重要意义。

**研究思路：**
针对点预测不足以刻画复杂环境不确定性的问题，UA-NWM将轨迹评分形式化为条件分布外检测，利用不确定性子空间表征多类可行未来，并将预测与目标间的差异分解为不确定性可解释部分与不可解释残差，仅以残差作为评分依据，从而无需多次采样即可实现鲁棒的轨迹选择。在仿真与真实无人机实验中，该方法在保持低推理延迟的同时，导航性能优于现有导航世界模型，验证了有效性与实用性。

**局限性：**
摘要未提供足够信息判断具体局限性。尽管真实无人机实验验证了可行性，但测试环境规模、气候条件、传感器设定及与大规模基线方法的对比细节尚未说明，跨场景泛化能力和不确定性建模的理论边界仍需进一步考察。

**值得关注：**
本文将轨迹评分转化为分布外检测的思路颇具启发性，为导航决策引入了不确定性建模与残差分解的新框架，可借鉴至地面机器人、自动驾驶等具身智能任务。其强调低推理延迟与真实平台验证的设计取向，也契合实时导航系统的实际部署需求，值得后续研究延续探索。

Deliberate Before You Fly: Vision-Guided Spatial Deliberation for UAV See-and-Reach Navigation

  • 作者Fanfu Xue, En Yu, Bohang Liu, Hongjun Wang, Yang Yang, Xindi Wang, Jiande Sun
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-08-05T13:27:07Z
  • 分类cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

UAV see-and-reach navigation requires an aerial agent to approach a language-specified target visible in its initial view and stop reliably near it. Existing methods typically map vision-language representations directly to action outputs without explicitly modeling intermediate fine-grained spatial decisions. This direct mapping causes semantic-control misalignment, leading to inconsistent maneuvers and unreliable termination. To address this issue, we propose DBFly, a vision-language waypoint prediction framework that introduces explicit vision-guided spatial deliberation before waypoint generation. Specifically, DBFly introduces a spatial maneuver decision chain that progressively performs target-direction anchoring, spatial diagnosis, and maneuver decision, enabling high-level maneuver intent to explicitly guide continuous waypoint generation. DBFly further constructs an implicit flight corridor by transforming the initial target-direction prior into a persistent geometric reference and deriving an online corridor state from the UAV's current position, thereby providing soft geometric guidance for spatial diagnosis and maneuver correction. In addition, DBFly develops a terminal-convergence-aware stopping strategy that characterizes terminal states through both target proximity and short-horizon motion convergence, enabling more reliable stopping near the target. Extensive experiments across seen, unseen-object, and unseen-scene test sets demonstrate that DBFly improves the success rate over the SOTA baseline by an average of 25.07 percentage points. The project homepage is available at https://xuefanfu.github.io/DBFly-Page.

Multi-Target Micro-Motion Parameter Estimation using MIMO-FMCW Radar with Limited Measurements

  • 作者Chandrashekhar Rai, Sanjay J. Alex, Arpan Chattopadhyay
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-08-05T10:26:50Z
  • 分类eess.SP
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

This work presents a compressive sensing-based approach for estimating the micro-motion parameters of targets with rotating components, such as small unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) with propellers, using fewer measurements than conventional methods. A multiple-input-multiple-output (MIMO) frequency-modulated continuous-wave (FMCW) radar employing a randomly spaced sparse linear antenna array is utilized. Random sequences of linear frequency-modulated (LFM) chirps are transmitted to enable random sampling in the slow-time domain. At first, the range, velocity, and angle of arrival (AoA) of the targets are estimated to identify the bulk motion. A three-dimensional point target response (3D-PTR) is then constructed using the estimated parameters and subtracted from the total radar return to extract the micro-Doppler signatures associated with target rotation. These residual signals are processed within a compressive sensing (CS) framework using the one-dimensional orthogonal matching pursuit (1D-OMP) algorithm to jointly estimate the propellers' rotation frequencies and blade lengths with the help of a parametric dictionary. The proposed approach is also extended to a multi-target scenario. Simulation results demonstrate that the proposed approach accurately estimates micro-motion parameters with limited measurements in the slow-time and the spatial dimensions, validating its potential for UAV detection applications. These estimated parameters are then used to distinguish among various UAV motion types using a classification framework based on a set of decision rules.

**研究背景：**
延迟容忍网络（DTN）在稀疏连接场景下依赖存储-携带-转发（SCF）机制，但间歇性接触、有限缓存和消息生存时间限制常导致投递率低、拥塞严重，端到端性能显著下降。现有路由协议如PRoPHET、MaxProp等虽广泛使用，却未能协同利用可控无人机（UAV）的机动性来主动创造更多未来接触机会。因此，如何将分散式机会路由与无人机航向控制联合优化，成为提升DTN性能的关键问题。

**研究思路：**
论文针对上述问题，提出联合优化去中心化机会路由与可控无人机飞行，通过离散航向扩大未来接触，并在接触受限观测下实现逐节点复制。具体地，将问题建模为因子化部分可观测马尔可夫决策过程（Dec-POMDP），采用集中训练与分散执行（CTDE）架构，提出基于近端策略优化（PPO）的JUROR方法：分散执行者依据局部观测决策，训练时评论者利用全局统计信息，并辅以可选的多时域热点预测器提供监督。模拟实验覆盖四种流量模式，结果显示JUROR相比PRoPHET和MaxProp均取得有效增益，同时保持了接触受限下的分散式执行能力。

**局限性：**
摘要中未提供足够信息判断具体局限性。当前验证仅基于仿真环境，未涉及真实无人机平台或实际无线信道；方法对热点预测器的依赖程度、扩展到大规模网络的可扩展性等也需进一步说明。

**值得关注：**
该研究将无人机运动控制与路由决策纳入统一的强化学习框架，采用CTDE范式解决了集中式全局信息与分布式执行之间的矛盾，对机器人通信网络、空地协同系统设计具有借鉴意义。其“运动-路由耦合”的问题建模方式，也可启发自动驾驶或具身智能中多智能体感知与行动协同策略的制定。

OutLangSplat：面向无人机户外场景的三维语言高斯泼溅

  • 来源arXiv

  • 分类cs.CV, cs.GR

**研究背景：**
三维语言高斯泼溅将开放词汇语言特征嵌入三维高斯表示，为文本驱动的三维场景理解提供了高效的显式表达。然而，现有方法多局限于室内或小规模场景，在无人机户外场景中往往失效：严重遮挡和远距离视角会导致语义激活错误、目标响应缺失。因此，如何让语言高斯表示适应无人机拍摄的大范围、多视角户外环境，成为亟待解决的问题。

**研究思路：**
针对上述问题，作者提出 OutLangSplat，从特征表示与特征聚合两方面提升可靠性。特征表示上，设计二维-三维双分支结构，结合区域对齐与融合以增强空间一致性，减少目标响应不完整和背景误激活；特征聚合上，提出无需训练的贡献与一致性感知聚合策略，利用像素贡献可靠性和跨视角语义一致性抑制噪声视角的干扰。论文还基于四个真实公开无人机数据集人工标注了多种物体，构建了首个面向无人机户外场景的开放词汇三维场景理解数据集。定量评估与消融实验表明，该方法在开放词汇语义分割和实例定位任务上均优于现有最先进方法。

**局限性：**
摘要中未提供足够信息判断具体局限性，例如对极端光照、动态物体或密集植被场景的鲁棒性，以及标注数据集规模对泛化能力的影响均未说明。

**值得关注：**
该工作将语言高斯表示拓展至无人机户外场景，提出了双分支特征表示和训练自由聚合策略，可迁移至机器人巡检、自动驾驶等大范围开放环境感知任务。首个可访问的无人机户外开放词汇数据集也为后续研究提供了统一评测基准。

Interpretable Fuzzy Inference for UAV Target Tracking Using Bounding-Box Geometry

  • 作者Reza Ahmari, Ahmad Mohammadi, Vahid Hemmati, Nicholas Edmond, Hossein Z. Saghazadeh, Olusola Odeyomi, Parham Kebria, Abdollah Homaifar
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-08-04T18:18:29Z
  • 分类cs.RO, cs.AI, cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Vision-based guidance of unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) toward unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs) supports cooperative aerial--ground robotics, but reliable continuous yaw estimation from onboard vision remains challenging because of sensing uncertainty, limited computation, and the need for interpretable control. Existing deep-learning and geometric-reconstruction approaches often require large datasets, external localization, or complex modeling assumptions, reducing transparency and deployment suitability on resource-constrained platforms. We present an interpretable fuzzy-inference framework that generates continuous yaw commands from low-dimensional features extracted from YOLO boxes: target centroid location, area, and aspect ratio. No explicit geometric modeling is required. A Mamdani fuzzy system serves as an interpretable baseline using a shoulder--triangle--shoulder input partition. It is followed by a first-order Takagi--Sugeno model with three antecedent membership terms per input, whose parameters are derived from training-set quantiles, yielding a compact 27-rule structure. Evaluation uses 6{,}169 labeled samples from a VICON motion-capture environment. Across five randomized train--test splits, the Takagi--Sugeno model achieves a test-set mean absolute error of $0.140^\circ \pm 0.003^\circ$, a root mean squared error of $0.200^\circ \pm 0.008^\circ$, and a maximum absolute error of $1.254^\circ \pm 0.121^\circ$. Within-threshold accuracies are $99.676% \pm 0.270%$ for $\pm1^\circ$ and $100.000% \pm 0.000%$ for both $\pm3^\circ$ and $\pm5^\circ$. Directional consistency between image-plane horizontal displacement and predicted yaw sign reaches $90.254% \pm 0.612%$. These results show that the framework is transparent, data-efficient, computationally lightweight, and suitable for real-time vision-based UAV guidance toward mobile ground targets.

LoRetta: A Foundation Model and Extensive Dataset for Global-Scale Remote Sensing Dense Image Matching

  • 作者Siwei Yu, Han Guo, Zhenwei Shi, Zhengxia Zou
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-08-04T18:02:19Z
  • 分类cs.CV, eess.IV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Dense image matching establishes pixel-wise correspondences and underpins broad applications in computer vision and photogrammetry. However, extending dense matching to global-scale remote sensing remains challenging because image pairs may differ in acquisition time, season, viewpoint, spatial resolution, and land-cover state. The resulting large geometric offsets, partial overlap, and intrinsically unmatchable regions make direct dense correspondence prediction unreliable and inefficient. We thus reformulate dense matching as localization-and-registration: first localizing the matchable overlap and affine geometry, then refining dense residuals within the aligned frame. Based on this formulation, we propose LoRetta, a foundation model coupling matchability-aware affine localization with guided dense registration. We also introduce LEVIR-GM, a global-scale multi-temporal optical matching benchmark with dataset-native matchability labels (103K aligned, 827K augmented pairs, six continents, five years, 0.5-1024 m resolution). We further establish a unified evaluation protocol for sparse, semi-dense, and dense matchers. On LEVIR-GM, LoRetta achieves an area under the curve (AUC) of 83.3%, outperforming the strongest baseline RoMa v2 by 1.6 points, with larger percentage of correct keypoints (PCK) gains of 6.5 and 8.2 points at 1 and 2 pixels, while reducing inference latency by 47.8%. Astronaut-to-satellite and unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV)-to-satellite geolocalization experiments further demonstrate its transferability as a reusable geometric aligner.
