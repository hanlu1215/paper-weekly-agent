EmbodiedIntentBench: Benchmarking Intent-Driven Runtime Adaptation for Mobile Robotic Missions

  • 作者Rajat Bhattacharjya, Changwoon Lee, Hyeonjong Ju, Sing-Yao Wu, Hamidreza Alikhani, Zhuoyi Yang, Ian Harris, Nalini Venkatasubramanian, Nikil Dutt
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-07T05:59:38Z
  • 分类cs.RO, cs.AR, cs.CV, cs.DC, cs.LG
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Mobile robotic sensing systems must adapt to operator requirements, limited resources, changing connectivity, and observation opportunities shaped by physical motion. Evaluating runtime strategies therefore requires assessing whether their decisions fulfill mission objectives, beyond isolated perception accuracy or execution efficiency. We present EmbodiedIntentBench, a benchmark for intent-driven runtime adaptation in mobile robotic missions. It represents mission intent through structured contracts for coverage, temporal evidence, reporting, urgency, and resources, and couples simulated unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) workloads with causal, resource-accounted execution. Computation decisions affect mission time, resource use, and which observations can contribute evidence. We evaluate fixed and adaptive perception strategies in multi-phase missions and under changing urgency, connectivity, and battery conditions. In the multi-phase Depot study, a configuration with 87.5% full-bank target recall across visible target observations completes only 1 of 16 missions, whereas a lower-recall configuration completes 14. In an ablation, ignoring execution-induced observation loss increases aggregate mission success and reverses the ordering of two runtime strategies. Replays using Jetson AGX Orin CPU and GPU profiles change the relative success of local and remote strategies. In selected Jetson GPU-profile replays, intent-driven adaptation reduces modeled task energy by up to 28% with unchanged mission success. EmbodiedIntentBench provides an extensible framework for evaluating runtime decisions against mission fulfillment under physical and resource constraints.

Mobility Information Capacity of Multi-Robot Systems: Safety-Aware Maneuver Design

  • 作者Weijie Yuan
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-06T22:47:03Z
  • 分类cs.RO, cs.IT, eess.SY
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Multiple robots operating in shared workspaces must adjust their speed, direction, and trajectories to coordinate with one another and respond to changing tasks and disturbances. The available maneuver freedom depends on the dynamics, uncertainty, and safety margins, even for a fixed fleet size and workspace. However, robot count, workspace occupancy, and task throughput do not quantify how much the fleet can vary its motion or how reliably these variations can be observed. We develop a mobility information capacity (MIC) framework that jointly characterizes these two aspects. Mobility information is defined as the mutual information between a joint maneuver sequence and its noisy trajectory observation, and MIC maximizes this quantity under maneuver-resource constraints. For a linear Gaussian maneuver-to-observation model with feedback dynamics, we characterize the finite-horizon information benchmark and derive its stationary long-horizon spectral limit, accounting for heterogeneous mobility gains, colored uncertainty, and temporally coupled maneuver costs. We further map workspace-boundary, inter-robot separation, and actuator chance constraints into coupled maneuver-covariance restrictions and derive conditions under which covariance optimization reduces to resource allocation across mobility modes. Finite joint maneuver codebooks are then constructed through individual risk screening and noise-whitened trajectory separation, with bounds linking sequence recovery error and achieved information to the capacity benchmarks. An aerial-robot simulation study evaluates spectral convergence, safety-aware allocation, finite-codebook identification, and fleet-size scaling. The resulting framework complements occupancy and task-throughput measures by quantifying the maximum observable maneuver freedom supported by a mobility system.

Zero-Shot Benchmarking of Unified Multimodal Models for Shallow-Water UAV Image Distortion Assessment and Restoration

  • 作者Dario Scilla, Omar A. Lopez, Mattia Rosso, Kasper Johansen, Matthew F. McCabe
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-06T17:12:08Z
  • 分类cs.CV
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Shallow-water UAV imagery provides centimeter-scale observations of benthic environments, but water-induced distortions complicate interpretation. To our knowledge, we present the first systematic zero-shot benchmark of unified multimodal large language models (MLLMs) for both assessing water-induced distortions and restoring shallow-water imagery within the same model framework. We evaluate five open-weight unified MLLMs with frozen pretrained weights and without access to paired reference images or descriptions of the true seabed. The benchmark contains 1,000 image pairs: 600 semi-synthetic UAV scenes created by applying synthetic water distortions to real shallow-water imagery and 400 fully synthetic through-water scenes. Three independent human annotations cover five distortion types. We evaluate four assessment prompts (P0-P3), from direct instruction to structured task decomposition, on all 1,000 images and four diagnosis-guided restoration prompts (R0-R3) on a common 100-image subset. Assessment varies substantially by model and prompt, with systematic prediction biases and a best pooled balanced accuracy of 0.654 for distortion presence. The best pooled PSNR gain is +0.255 dB, no unified MLLM improves pooled LPIPS, and some outputs alter scene structure or hallucinate content. Two restoration-only baselines also show limited transfer: InstructIR achieves a pooled PSNR gain of +0.040 dB, while PromptFix degrades all four pooled metrics under every prompt. These results show that recognizing shallow-water degradation does not imply reliable physical restoration and provide a reproducible baseline for future work.

**研究背景：**
无人机自主三维探索需在有限飞行时间与机载算力下选择目标。传统基于欧氏距离的前沿流程在杂乱环境中难以反映实际无碰撞行程成本，可能选到低效前沿；因此需要更贴近可飞行代价、又轻量的重排序方法，以提升探索效率并兼顾建图质量。

**研究思路：**
针对上述偏差，作者先用轻量效用函数保留少量top-N候选前沿，再用时间受限RRT*估计其可行路径成本并重排序，且将选中前沿的路径复用于导航以减少重复全局规划。论文在三个仓库类环境中与常规前沿基线比较，报告探索轨迹最多缩短14.1%，两个环境探索时间最多减少9.5%，第三个保持竞争性，地图细节与OctoMap基线相当。

**局限性：**
摘要仅说明三个仓库类环境实验，未提供真实飞行、动态障碍、不同传感器配置或跨场景泛化验证；top-N规模、时间预算和效用函数参数对性能与实时性的影响也未说明，故具体局限及机载部署稳定性仍需更多证据判断。

**值得关注：**
该工作把前沿选择从几何距离转向“候选筛选+有界路径规划”的可行代价评估，并复用路径降低全局规划开销，为LiDAR无人机探索提供了可借鉴的轻量化重排序范式；其以轨迹长度、探索时间和地图细节共同评价的思路，也适合后续具身探索系统权衡效率与建图质量。

Pinching Antenna Systems (PASS) for Air-Ground Integrated Sensing and Communications (ISAC)

  • 作者Chaoran Chen, Haochen Li, Jincheng Zhu, Zhiwen Pan, Mark F. Flanagan
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-06T13:05:58Z
  • 分类eess.SP
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

This paper proposes a multi-user single-target air-ground integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) system, in which a ground pinching-antenna system (PASS) transmits communication and sensing signals and an unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) receives the target echo. The communication and sensing beamforming vectors, pinching antenna positions, and UAV position are jointly optimized to minimize the weighted Cramér-Rao bound (CRB). A penalty dual decomposition (PDD) algorithm is developed for the resulting nonconvex problem. The auxiliary variable and beamforming subproblems admit globally optimal solutions in semi-closed form, while the positions of the pinching antennas and the UAV are updated by successive convex approximation (SCA). Under mild conditions, every accumulation point of the sequence generated by our proposed algorithm is a Karush-Kuhn-Tucker (KKT) point of the reformulated problem. Numerical results show that the proposed PASS-UAV design achieves a lower weighted CRB than conventional multiple-input multiple-output (MIMO) and PASS benchmarks.

Task-Driven Closed-Loop Design Optimization of Passively Morphing Quadrotors for Contact-Mediated Narrow Gap Traversal

  • 作者Etor Arza, Luca Girardi, Gabriel Maquignaz, Javier Paez, Stefan Leutenegger, Stefano Mintchev, Kostas Alexis
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-06T10:27:02Z
  • 分类cs.RO
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Aerial robots operating in cluttered environments may encounter openings narrower than their nominal width. Existing passively morphing drones reduce their width upon traversal, but they typically involve human-made designs, which can struggle to find the right balance among folding ratio, flight efficiency, and control tracking performance. We introduce a closed-loop design method that jointly optimizes quadrotor geometry and the torque-deflection response of two passively folding arms driven by the success rate in simulation for contact-mediated gap traversal. An optimization algorithm samples candidate designs that are evaluated in a contact-based simulation of the traversal task, where the task success rate is used as a feedback signal to sample progressively better designs over time. The resulting prototype passively reduces its width from 31.3 to 14.1 cm and traverses a 15 cm wide opening at a 2 m/s reference speed. It also tolerates gap approach-angle errors of up to 30 degrees and folds through a 40 cm long confined passage. Compared to a rigid twin, the soft configuration adds only 2 mm of position RMSE and less than 1% electrical power during contact-free flight. These results show that task-driven closed-loop design optimization can produce passively morphing airframes that are compact under contact, tolerant to approach error, and effectively rigid during nominal flight.

Capacity of Multi-Layer Manhattan-Grid UAV Corridors Served by Terrestrial 5G NR Networks

  • 作者Saad Masrur, M M Sadman Shafi, Md Sharif Hossen, Ismail Guvenc
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-06T05:49:16Z
  • 分类eess.SP, cs.PF
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Advanced air mobility (AAM) is expected to organize unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) traffic into corridors at multiple altitudes. In this paper, we study the downlink (DL) and uplink (UL) capacity of a multi-layer Manhattan-grid corridor mesh served by a terrestrial fifth-generation (5G) new radio (NR) macro network. The corridor spacing and the numbers of layers, UAVs, and base stations are configurable. We combine the 3GPP aerial channel and antenna models, correlated UAV mobility, and frequency-selective fading with an NR physical-layer abstraction that includes channel aging and link adaptation. Results show that UAVs above 90 m experience a DL signal-to-interference-plus-noise ratio (SINR) about 17 dB below that of ground users, and that a dense macro network with $10^\circ$ downtilt leaves 17-29% of corridor positions without DL service under full-buffer load, regardless of corridor spacing. On a common layout, the carrier frequency (700 MHz, 3.5 GHz, or 7.2 GHz) changes the UAV SINR by less than 0.5 dB, since the corridor links are strongly interference-limited. The UL is the bottleneck: at 85% satisfaction, a 20 MHz carrier supports only about 3.4-4.1 UAVs/km$^2$ with 4 Mb/s of UL video per UAV.

UAV-Based Computer Vision for Cattle Detection and Feed Bunk Spatial Analysis

  • 作者Pedro Oliveira Torrente, Diego Giaretta de Paulo, Lucas Pascotti Valem, Jianglong Yan, Everton Castelão Tetila, Jayme Garcia Arnal Barbedo, Liang Zhao
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-06T03:18:45Z
  • 分类cs.CV

  • PDF 链接

Beef cattle production is central to Brazilian agribusiness and faces growing demands for efficiency, sustainability, and animal welfare. Precision Livestock Farming (PLF) offers tools to address these challenges, particularly through monitoring feeding-related spatial patterns in confined systems. Feeding management plays a decisive role in animal performance, yet its evaluation still relies largely on subjective, labor-intensive visual inspections that are hard to scale and prone to bias. This work proposes a computer vision framework based on Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs) to quantify cattle alignment and spatial dispersion at the feed bunk under real-world feedlot conditions. A UAV dataset was collected during flight campaigns at a commercial feedlot in Mato Grosso do Sul, Brazil. Images were manually annotated into cattle head and cattle back classes to support object detection and image-level spatial analysis of cattle distribution. You Only Look Once (YOLO) models, namely YOLOv8 and YOLOv11, were evaluated, with YOLOv8 small used for the downstream spatial analysis. The feed bunk region was segmented using the Segment Anything Model (SAM), and geometric metrics based on detected head positions were developed to characterize alignment, spacing regularity, and clustering. The selected detector achieved 84.30% mAP50 and 35.50% mAP50-95, with an inference time of approximately 2.7 ms per image on an NVIDIA RTX 5060 Ti GPU. The resulting metrics distinguished representative regular and irregular spatial distributions by Root Mean Square Deviation - RMSD (1.06 m and 4.26 m), Coefficient of Variation - CV (42.70% and 96.31%), and clustering (0 and 2 clusters), respectively. These findings indicate that the proposed approach can provide indicators for assessing feed bunk spatial patterns in PLF.

  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-05T20:07:33Z
  • 分类eess.SP
  • 论文链接

As integrated sensing and communication (ISAC) lets 5G and 6G base stations detect drones, software agents will soon act on what they sense. This amplifies the threat of a relay replaying the base station's signal as a phantom drone. For such cognitive ISAC systems, this paper proposes a wall-based physical check. A digital twin (DT) predicts where a real target's wall echo must arrive; a relay can fake range, but not where its own reflections come from. The paper proves that line-of-sight beam steering cannot expose a matched relay, and that, under stated conditions, a single-antenna relay faking a wall echo leaves a telltale echo where a genuine target leaves none. In a pre-registered ray-tracing study, the twin's evidence separated genuine targets from replays perfectly (AUC 1.000) on held-out streets, and a later-registered passive check needing no extra transmission accepted every genuine target and no replay. The primary active probe was inconclusive, at 5.3\% false acceptances against a 5\% limit, for lack of threshold margin. At the evaluated relay positions, the conditional bound certifies 3 of 24 held-out streets, reproduced on fresh ones, against single-antenna relay signals meeting its conditions, adaptive or fixed, and 11 of 24 at double bandwidth.
