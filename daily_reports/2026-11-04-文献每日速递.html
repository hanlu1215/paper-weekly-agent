**研究思路：**

A Tightly Coupled Magnetic-Inertial SLAM System for Field Robots

  • 作者Chuan Huang, Tongxing Peng
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-02T13:23:39Z
  • 分类cs.RO, eess.SP
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Field robots operating in mines, tunnels, and disaster areas require reliable localization despite GNSS denial and degraded camera and LiDAR measurements caused by dust, smoke, or poor illumination. Missions involving both ground and aerial robots further motivate complementary sensing modalities that do not depend on visibility, preinstalled infrastructure, or wheel contact. This letter presents MISLAM, a tightly coupled magnetic-inertial simultaneous localization and mapping system using an IMU and a nine-magnetometer array, without relying on external odometry or platform-specific motion assumptions. The system combines the inertial navigation model with local polynomial magnetic-field models for short-term motion estimation and a global Gaussian process model for drift correction using an error-state Kalman filter. Offline evaluation on three indoor datasets collected with a handheld rig shows reductions in total endpoint closure error of 45.7% - 78.0% relative to magnetic-inertial odometry. Under poor illumination, MISLAM maintains tracking after visual-inertial odometry fails, achieving a horizontal closure error of 0.332 m, although vertical drift remains a limitation. These results support magnetic-inertial SLAM as a complementary localization modality for robotic sensor suites operating under degraded visual conditions.

Deep Reinforcement Learning for BD-RIS-Assisted Multi-UAV RSMA Communications

  • 作者Aamer Mohamed Huroon, Ali A. Nasir, Daniel B. da Costa
  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-02T12:44:45Z
  • 分类cs.IT, cs.ET
  • 论文链接
  • PDF 链接

Beyond-diagonal reconfigurable intelligent surfaces (BD-RISs) extend conventional diagonal RISs by allowing controllable coupling among reflecting elements. This flexibility is attractive for aerial networks, where unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) face rapidly varying channels and strict power limits. In this paper, we study a hybrid BD-RIS-assisted multi-UAV rate-splitting multiple access (RSMA) downlink, in which UAVs within favourable BD-RIS coverage use both direct and BD-RIS-aided links, whereas the remaining UAVs are served through direct links only. We formulate a network sum-rate maximization problem that jointly optimizes UAV trajectories, RSMA common-rate allocation, beamforming, BD-RIS scattering matrices, and UAV-BD-RIS association. The resulting mixed-integer non-convex problem is solved by using deep deterministic policy gradient (DDPG) algorithm, equipped with a constraint-preserving action layer that generates reciprocal and lossless BD-RIS responses in closed form and handles the binary association inside the environment transition. Simulation results, averaged over three training seeds under a physically consistent channel model with blocked direct links near the BD-RISs, show that the proposed design attains a sum rate comparable to SAC and higher than twin delayed deep deterministic policy gradient ( TD3), proximal policy optimization (PPO), and deep Q-network (DQN), exceeds per-slot alternating optimization with fixed trajectories at about 1/65 of its per-slot runtime, and benefits mainly from RSMA, while the additional BD-RIS gain is modest.

  • 来源arXiv
  • 发布时间2026-11-02T12:16:32Z

This paper presents a decentralized guiding vector field framework for rigid formation path following in multi- UAV systems based on the superposition of simple rule-driven vector fields. Each control objective is encoded as a dedicated vector-field component: a normalized tangential term to ensure progression along a prescribed geometric path at a desired speed, a convergence term driving the formation toward the path, a local coordination term that preserves the desired formation shape through a pseudo-path parameter, and a local separation term for inter-agent collision avoidance. The coordination mechanism requires the exchange of only a single scalar per agent, resulting in low communication bandwidth. Partial theoretical results establish convergence of the swarm to the desired path and formation in the absence of the sepa- ration term, while collision avoidance is formally guaranteed. The approach is experimentally validated using a swarm of five quadrotors executing two 3D choreographies. The first experiment involves tracking a helical path while maintaining a rigid V-shaped formation. The second demonstrates a spiral- like motion obtained by a rotating circular formation tracking a vertical line. The experimental results support the effectiveness and practical applicability of the proposed framework.

  • 分类cs.RO, eess.SY, math.OC

This paper studies dual path following for a quadrotor carrying a three-degree-of-freedom arm. The center of mass of the combined system must follow a vehicle path and the end effector must follow a second path, while the arm respects its joint limits and avoids its singular postures. The vehicle and the arm are mechanically coupled, so one controller must handle both paths. With the center of mass and the end-effector path coordinates as outputs, and a dynamic extension of the thrust, the system admits a normal form without internal dynamics. We characterize the states where this normal form fails. We formulate the transverse feedback linearizing controller as a quadratic program that relaxes only the speed of the end effector along its path and imposes safety and actuator limits as inequality constraints. The program reduces to a scalar problem with a closed-form solution. If it remains strictly feasible for all time, the feedback is locally Lipschitz, both paths are invariant and attractive, and every constraint holds. In the physics engine Drake, the controller resolves a conflict between the two speed references by slowing the end effector while both paths remain invariant.
